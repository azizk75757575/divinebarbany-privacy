Cast to TV: Screen Mirroring – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Cast to TV lets you mirror your phone screen and play your own videos, photos, music and links on a TV, Chromecast, DLNA device or web browser on your home network.

Your photos, videos and music

You choose the files to cast with Android's own photo picker or file picker; the app only gets access to the files you pick, for as long as it is open. To play them, the app runs a small web server on your phone, inside your own Wi-Fi network, and the TV you selected downloads the file directly from your phone. Your files are never uploaded to us or to any other server, and the app keeps no copies or history of them. Links you paste are sent only to the TV you chose.

Screen mirroring

Screen Mirroring opens your phone's own wireless display feature (Miracast, Smart View, Cast); the app does not see the mirrored content. Browser Mirror captures your screen only after you accept Android's screen-capture prompt, and only while the "Screen mirroring is on" notification is shown. The picture is sent only to web browsers on your local network that open the address shown in the app; it is not recorded or stored and never leaves your network through us. Stop it at any time from the app or the notification.

Finding your TV

To find TVs, the app sends standard discovery messages (SSDP/UPnP and Google Cast) on your local network and reads the TV names and models that answer. TVs you add by IP address are saved on your device only.

Advertising

Cast to TV is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google Cast

When you cast to a Chromecast or a TV with Chromecast built-in, Google Play services handle the connection to that device, under Google's privacy policy.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

Cast to TV has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or unlock HD browser mirroring for the current session, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet, network and Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast – to find TVs on your Wi-Fi, to let the TV download your media from your phone, and to load ads.
  • Foreground service (media playback, screen capture) and notifications – to keep casting or Browser Mirror running with the screen off and to show that it is active, with a Stop button.
  • Wake lock – to keep the Wi-Fi connection awake while the TV is playing.

The app does not request access to your whole photo library, contacts, messages, call log, microphone, camera or location.

Children

Cast to TV is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
