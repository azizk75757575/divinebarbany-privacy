Privacy Policy - ProofSpot: GPS Camera & Proof (Android)

Effective date: 19 October 2026. Publisher: AlgoTechApps. App: com.divinebarbany.proofspot on Google Play.

ProofSpot is a camera that stamps photos and videos with the time and place and signs them on your phone so they can be verified. This page explains what the app handles and why.

Summary

  • Your photos, videos, locations, notes, reports and signing key stay on your phone. We have no servers and no user accounts.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from the IP address) and ad interaction data, as described below.
  • We never sell your data.

Camera, microphone and sensors

The camera and microphone are used only while you take a photo or record a video. Captures are saved in the app's private storage and, if you keep the option on, a copy is added to your phone's gallery (Pictures/ProofSpot or Movies/ProofSpot). When you verify a photo, Android's photo picker or file picker gives the app only the file you choose; it is checked on the phone and not uploaded. Motion sensors provide the camera tilt and compass direction (to retake a photo from the same position and angle), the magnetic field and the air pressure shown on stamps.

Location

Your precise location is used only while the app is open, to stamp and sign your captures, to show the map on the stamp and to guide you back to a saved spot. It is stored with your captures on your phone. The app never uses location in the background.

To show a street address, the app uses Android's geocoding service (provided by Google on most phones), which receives the coordinates. To draw the small maps on stamps and reports, the app downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) and, for satellite and hybrid maps, from Esri (server.arcgisonline.com); the tiles requested reveal the area being shown, at a street-level scale, and your IP address, as for any web request. If you turn on weather, the position rounded to about 1 km is sent to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) to get the current weather; nothing else is sent.

Signatures and verification

Each capture is signed with a key created in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore, StrongBox when available). The private key cannot be read or exported, not even by the app. The QR code printed on a capture contains the signed time, position, GPS accuracy, compass direction, a fingerprint of the picture and the public part of the key. Anyone who receives the photo can read this information. Scanning the code opens a verification page that checks it inside the browser: the code after the "#" in the link is not sent to any server. Do not share a photo if you do not want its place and time to be known.

Reports and exports

PDF reports, CSV and KML files are created on your phone. You choose where they go when you share them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ProofSpot shows ads provided by Google AdMob, never on the camera screen. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, crash and diagnostic data, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google > Ads). Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Watching a rewarded video unlocks ProofSpot Pro for 24 hours, during which no ads are shown. Your photos and locations are never used for advertising.

Purchases

Nothing is sold in the app. ProofSpot Pro is unlocked for 24 hours by watching a rewarded video.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Copies you saved to the gallery or shared stay where you put them. Since we keep no data on servers, there is nothing for us to delete; Google's ad data is handled under Google's policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
