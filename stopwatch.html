Stopwatch & Timer: Multi Timer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Stopwatch & Timer is a stopwatch with laps, a countdown timer, a multi timer, an interval (Tabata) timer, a full-screen stopwatch and a floating stopwatch window.

Your times, laps and history

Your running stopwatches and timers, their names, your laps, your saved history and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. You can delete history in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes everything. When you use "Share", the text is sent only to the app you choose.

Alarms and notifications

The app uses Android's exact alarm function so that a timer can ring at the right second, even when the app is closed. It shows notifications for timers that reach zero and for clocks that keep running while the app is closed. Notifications stay on your device. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Voice announcements

Voice announcements (laps, time left, "Time's up") use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. The app does not use the microphone and does not record anything.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob inside the app (never in the floating window). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. You can unlock the Multi timer until you close the app, or turn ads off for 24 hours, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to ring when a timer reaches zero and to show running clocks. You can refuse it; timers then ring only while the app is open.
  • Alarms (exact alarms) – to make timers ring on time with the app closed.
  • Run at startup – to set your running timers again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration – to vibrate when a timer rings.
  • Internet and network state – to load ads.

The app does not request access to your photos, files, contacts, messages, call log, camera, microphone or location, and does not need the "display over other apps" permission (the floating stopwatch uses Android's picture-in-picture window).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
