Auto Clicker: Automatic Tap – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore)

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Auto Clicker performs automatic taps and swipes on your screen at the places and times you choose, from a floating control panel.

Accessibility Service

Android only lets an app tap and swipe on other apps through the Accessibility Service API. Auto Clicker uses this API for two things only: to perform the taps, long presses and swipes that you place on the screen when you press play, and to show its floating panel and targets over other apps.

  • The service does not read, record or store the content of your screen (it is declared with canRetrieveWindowContent="false").
  • It does not record what you type and ignores all accessibility events.
  • It does not collect, store or share any personal or sensitive data. Nothing obtained through this API leaves your phone.
  • The app shows an explanation and asks for your agreement before it sends you to the Accessibility settings. You can turn the service off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings.

Recording

In record mode, the app keeps the positions and timing of the taps and swipes you make while recording, so it can play them back. It does not capture images of the screen. Recordings stay on your phone.

Saved setups and settings

Your saved setups (target positions, delays, stop conditions) and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

No ads, no purchases

The Amazon Appstore version of Auto Clicker shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK and has no in-app purchases.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. No data from the app is sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Accessibility Service – to perform your taps and swipes and show the floating panel, as described above. You turn it on yourself.
  • Internet and network state – only to open this privacy policy and the store page when you tap them.
  • Vibration – a short buzz when a run starts and stops (can be turned off in Settings).

The app does not request access to your photos, files, contacts, messages, call log, camera, microphone or location, and does not need the "display over other apps" permission.

Children

Auto Clicker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
