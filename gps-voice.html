GPS Voice Navigation: Live Map – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

GPS Voice Navigation shows your position on a live map, finds places and routes, and speaks turn-by-turn directions.

Location

The app uses your device's location only while the app is open on screen: to show you on the map, to calculate a route from where you are, to find places near you and to follow your progress during voice navigation. The app never uses your location in the background and does not keep a history of where you have been.

To find places and calculate routes, the app sends your search text and the start and end coordinates of a route (or your position, for nearby places) to these OpenStreetMap-based services, which answer with the results:

  • Place search: Photon by Komoot (photon.komoot.io)
  • Routes: FOSSGIS OSRM routing servers (routing.openstreetmap.de)
  • Nearby places: Overpass API (overpass-api.de)
  • Map images: OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org)

These requests contain no name, account or advertising identifier. The services receive your IP address as part of any internet request, under their own policies.

Voice

When you tap the microphone to say a destination, your speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service installed on your device (for example Google's speech service), under that provider's privacy policy. The app only receives the recognized text and does not record or store audio. Spoken directions are produced by your device's text-to-speech engine.

Saved places and parking spot

Saved places, recent searches and your saved parking spot are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them. When you choose Share, the app passes the place name and a map link to the app you pick.

Advertising

GPS Voice is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. Voice navigation is unlocked for one trip by watching a short rewarded video; no ad is shown over the map while you are being guided. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The app does not pass your precise GPS location to the ads SDK.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no servers of its own and no analytics of its own.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate) – to show you on the map and guide you, only while the app is open.
  • Internet and network state – to load the map, search places, calculate routes, and load ads and the consent message.

Safety

Directions are a guide only. Always follow traffic laws, road signs and road conditions, and do not handle your phone while driving. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Children

GPS Voice is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
