Privacy Policy - Ring Burst: Rotate Ring Puzzle

Effective date: 25 November 2026. Developer: AlgoTechApps.

Ring Burst is a free ring puzzle game for Android. This page explains what information the game handles and why.

What we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no account system and no analytics of its own, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your progress (levels, coins, rockets, Daily Challenge streak and sound settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC): banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded videos (for example to use the Hammer or to double a level reward). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent message before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy (shield) button on the game screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Coins and prizes

Coins, rockets and other rewards exist only inside the game. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. The game has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state - only to load ads and the consent message. The puzzles work offline.
  • Advertising ID - used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Inside the game: the privacy (shield) button, shown where consent is required by law.
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
