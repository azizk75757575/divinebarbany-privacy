Period Calendar: Cycle Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Period Calendar: Cycle Tracker helps you log your periods, predicts your next period, fertile window and ovulation day, lets you note symptoms and mood, and estimates with Plan Check the chance that your period overlaps a plan you add (a trip, a wedding, an exam…).

Your cycle data stays on your device

Your period days, cycle and period lengths, symptoms, moods, notes, plans, reminder settings and optional PIN are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, they are never shared with advertisers or the ad SDK, and they are not used for advertising. The app has no account and no cloud sync. You can erase everything at any time with Settings > Delete all data; uninstalling the app or clearing its data also deletes it. If you use Android's device backup, Android may include this data in your own Google backup.

The optional PIN is stored only as a salted hash. Predictions are estimates. The app is not a medical device, does not give medical advice and must not be used for contraception.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your health and cycle data is never shared with the ad SDK.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. You can open Plan Check for 15 minutes, or turn ads off for 24 hours, by choosing to watch a short rewarded video. These time windows are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show the reminders you turn on (period coming, fertile window, ovulation, plan alerts, daily log). Discreet mode keeps their text neutral.
  • Run at startup – to set your daily reminder again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Tracking and reminders work offline.

The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data from other apps, and it does not use Health Connect.

Children

Period Calendar: Cycle Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
