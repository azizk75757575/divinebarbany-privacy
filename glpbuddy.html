Privacy Policy - GLP Buddy: GLP-1 Tracker

Effective date: 22 November 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

GLP Buddy is a personal tracking app for people who use weekly GLP-1 medications. It is available for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play) and is designed so that your information stays on your device. This page explains what the app handles and why.

Summary

  • We do not collect any personal or health data. We have no servers and no user accounts.
  • iPhone version: there are no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.
  • Android version: the app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (see Advertising below). Your health entries are never shared with the ad SDK.
  • Nothing you enter is sold, shared or used for marketing.

Information you enter

Injections, doses, injection sites, weight, protein and water intake, side effects, daily check-ins, pen supply and refill costs, insurance and coverage dates (Android), notes and your goals are stored only on your device, in the app's local database. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. You can delete everything at any time from Settings > Delete all data, or by deleting the app.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you turn on Sync with Apple Health, GLP Buddy asks for permission to read and write three data types: body mass (weight), dietary water and dietary protein. Data read from Apple Health is used only to show your charts and daily totals inside the app. Data the app writes to Apple Health is only the values you log. HealthKit data never leaves your device through GLP Buddy, is not stored in iCloud by the app, is not used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing > Apps > GLP Buddy.

Advertising (Google Play version)

The Android version of GLP Buddy is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. All ad requests are for non-personalised ads, and ad content is limited to a general-audience rating. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The ad SDK never receives your injections, weight, symptoms, coverage details or any other entry you make in the app.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app shows Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos (Google Play version)

The Android version has no in-app purchases. You can unlock the Pro tools (PDF report, shot-cycle map, symptom trends and share card) for 24 hours by watching a short rewarded video. The unlock time is stored only on your device.

Android data storage

On Android, your entries are stored in the app's private storage. Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this data, so it is not copied to Google Drive. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Notifications

Injection-day, refill, coverage-expiry, water and protein reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are never sent from a server and never contain marketing. You can turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

Face ID / fingerprint / App Lock

If you enable App Lock, the operating system verifies your Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, face unlock or device PIN. The app only receives a success or failure result; biometric data is never accessible to GLP Buddy.

PDF reports and share cards

Reports and progress cards are generated on your device. They are shared only if you choose to share them, through the standard system share sheet, with the recipients you pick.

Subscriptions (iPhone version)

GLP Buddy Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. Subscription status is checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit.

Review requests

Occasionally the app asks Apple (App Store) or Google (Play In-App Review) to show the standard rating prompt. This is handled by the store; we receive no personal data from it.

Medical disclaimer

GLP Buddy is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or dosing recommendations. Always follow the instructions of your prescriber.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
