Storage Analyzer & Cleaner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Storage Analyzer & Cleaner shows how the storage of your Android device is used, folder by folder and file by file, and helps you delete the files you no longer need: large files, duplicate files, APK installers, old downloads and empty folders.

Your files

To measure the size of every folder and to delete the files you select, the app asks for Android's “All files access” permission (on Android 10 and older: the storage permission). With it, the app reads the names, sizes and dates of the files and folders on your device and on any SD card or USB drive, and creates small previews of photos, videos and APK files.

All of this happens only on your device. Your files, their names, their contents and the results of the analysis are never uploaded to us or to any other server, never shared, and never used for advertising. The duplicate finder compares file contents locally on your device. The only thing the app saves is a short list of folder counts from your last scan, so the next scan can show an accurate percentage; it stays in the app's private storage on your device.

Deleting files

The app deletes a file or folder only after you select it and confirm. Deleted items are first moved to a hidden folder on the same storage so you can undo the cleanup from the result screen; when you leave that screen they are deleted permanently. You can withdraw the “All files access” permission at any time in your Android settings.

Opening and sharing

When you tap “Open” or “Share”, the file is handed only to the app you choose, through Android's standard sharing system.

Advertising

Storage Analyzer & Cleaner is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The ads SDK does not receive your files or the results of the analysis.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a short rewarded video. This setting, and the total space you have freed with the app, are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • All files access / storage – to measure your storage and delete the files you choose, on your device only.
  • Internet and network state – only to load ads and the consent message.

The app does not request access to your contacts, messages, call log, microphone, camera or location.

Children

Storage Analyzer & Cleaner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
