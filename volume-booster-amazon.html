Volume Booster: Sound & Bass – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore)

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps · This policy covers the version of the app distributed on the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets.

Volume Booster makes the sound of your device louder. It adds a volume boost, a 5-band equalizer, a bass booster, 3D surround and a volume mixer for the media, ringtone, notification, alarm and system volumes.

Summary

This version of the app has no ads, no analytics and no account. We (the developer) do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Audio

Volume Booster uses Android's own audio effects (loudness enhancer, equalizer, bass boost and virtualizer) to change how sound plays on your device. The app does not record, listen to, store or send any audio. It does not use the microphone and does not read which songs, videos or apps you play.

Background service and notification

While the booster is on, the app runs a foreground service with a visible notification, so that your boost and equalizer keep working in other apps. You start and stop it yourself in the app, in the notification or with the Quick Settings tile. The notification is shown on your device only.

Settings stored on your device

Your boost level, equalizer settings and volume choices are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.

In-app purchase

Super Boost (boost above 50%), Bass Booster and 3D Surround can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase ("PRO"). The purchase is handled entirely by Amazon through the Amazon Appstore; we never see your payment details. The app only receives from Amazon whether the purchase is owned, and keeps that on your device. Amazon's handling of your data is described in the Amazon Privacy Notice.

Permissions

  • Modify audio settings – to apply the boost and equalizer and to change the volumes in the volume mixer.
  • Foreground service and notifications – to keep the boost working while you use other apps and to show the control notification.
  • Internet – only used by the Amazon in-app purchasing service. The app itself sends nothing.

The app does not request access to your microphone, location, photos, contacts, messages, call log or camera.

Hearing safety

Listening at a high volume for a long time can damage your hearing and small speakers. The app shows a warning the first time you go above 60%.

Children

Volume Booster is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
