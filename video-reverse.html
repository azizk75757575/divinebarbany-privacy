Reverse Video - Reverse Audio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Reverse Video - Reverse Audio ("Video Reverse") plays videos and sounds backwards, makes boomerang loops and offers the Reverse Challenge, a game where you copy your own voice in reverse. All processing happens on your device.

Your files

You choose the videos and audio files yourself with the system photo or file picker. The app only reads the files you select, only to reverse them, and never uploads them. Results are saved on your device in the Movies/Video Reverse and Music/Video Reverse folders, where you can play, rename, share or delete them. The app does not download videos from the internet.

Microphone (Reverse Challenge)

The microphone is used only inside the Reverse Challenge, only while you are recording, and only after you allow it. Your recordings are reversed and compared with each other on your device to compute your score. They are never uploaded, never shared with us or with third parties, and are kept only in the app's temporary storage on your phone. If you tap "Create share video", a video of your recordings is saved to your Movies/Video Reverse folder, and it is shared only if you choose an app to share it with.

Advertising

Video Reverse is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

Video Reverse has no in-app purchases. A Reverse Challenge round is unlocked by watching a short rewarded video, and you can turn ads off for 24 hours the same way. These settings and your best challenge score are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Reversing works offline.
  • Microphone – Reverse Challenge only, as described above. Everything stays on your device.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to save results in the Movies and Music folders.

The app does not request access to your photos, videos or music library: it only sees the files you pick.

Children

Video Reverse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
