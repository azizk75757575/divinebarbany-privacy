Voice Screen Lock: AI Lock – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Voice Screen Lock shows a custom lock screen that you unlock by saying your voice password, or with a backup PIN or pattern. It works on top of Android's own screen lock and does not replace it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while the voice password screen or the lock screen is listening: when you tap the mic, or when the lock screen appears and "Listen automatically" is on. The app does not record or store any audio. Speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service installed on your device (for example Google's speech service), which may process it on the device or, depending on your device and language, on that provider's servers under its own privacy policy. The app only receives the recognized text and compares it with your saved phrase on your device. The app never listens in the background.

Your voice password, PIN and pattern

Your voice phrase, backup PIN and pattern are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The PIN and pattern are stored as a salted one-way hash. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them. The app recognizes the words you say, not your voiceprint, and does not create any biometric data.

Wallpaper photo

If you choose your own photo as the lock screen wallpaper, you pick it with Android's photo picker. The app keeps a resized copy in its private storage on your device to show it on the lock screen. It is never uploaded.

Advertising

Voice Screen Lock is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob inside the app's own screens (never on the lock screen). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period

Voice Screen Lock has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or unlock extra wallpapers, by watching a short rewarded video. These choices are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above and the speech processing done by your device's speech recognition service.

Permissions

  • Microphone – to hear your voice password while the lock screen or setup screen is listening.
  • Display over other apps – so the lock screen can appear when the screen turns on.
  • Foreground service and notifications – while Voice Lock is turned on, a small notification shows that it is active; the service only waits for the screen to turn off to show the lock screen.
  • Run at startup – to turn Voice Lock back on after a restart, if you had it on.
  • Vibration – optional vibration when you unlock.
  • Internet – only to load ads and the consent message.

Children

Voice Screen Lock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
