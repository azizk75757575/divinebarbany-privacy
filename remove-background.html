AI Remove Background & Object – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

AI Remove Background & Object (shown on your phone as "Remove BG") removes the background from photos you choose, lets you change the background, erase objects, add shadows with Smart Blend, make product photos and stickers, and save the result on your device. All image processing happens on your device.

Your photos

You choose photos yourself with the Android photo picker (or by sharing a photo to the app). The app only reads the photos you select, only to edit them, and never uploads them to any server. Pictures you save are stored on your device in the Pictures/RemoveBG folder, where you can view, share or delete them from the app or your gallery.

On-device AI

Background removal uses Google ML Kit subject segmentation, which runs entirely on your device. Its AI model is downloaded once by Google Play services. ML Kit does not send your photos anywhere; Google Play services may send basic diagnostic and usage information about the ML Kit API, as described in Google's ML Kit terms.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. Smart Blend is unlocked for the current photo, and ads can be turned off for 24 hours, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Removing backgrounds works offline once the AI model is installed.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to save your pictures in the Pictures folder.

The app does not request access to your photo library: it only sees the photos you pick.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
