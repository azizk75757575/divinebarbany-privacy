Emoji Battery Status Bar & Pet – Privacy Policy (HUAWEI AppGallery)

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · HUAWEI AppGallery version · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Emoji Battery lets you replace your battery icon with a cute emoji battery, customize your status bar, decorate the front camera (notch), show screen pets and a charging animation, and add a battery widget to your home screen.

Accessibility Service

Android allows only accessibility services to draw over the system status bar, so Emoji Battery uses the Accessibility Service API for one purpose: to draw your emoji battery, custom status bar, notch decoration, screen pets and charging animation on top of other apps. Before you turn it on, the app explains this and asks you to agree.

  • The service does not read, collect, store or share the content of your screen, your messages, your notifications, your passwords or anything you type. It is configured without permission to retrieve window content.
  • It only uses the name (package) of the app that is open, to hide the overlays in the apps you choose and while the notification shade is open. This information stays in memory on your device and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • It does not perform any action for you, does not change your settings and does not track what you do.
  • You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility > Emoji Battery.

Battery, Wi-Fi and signal information

To draw the battery, the clock and the Wi-Fi and mobile signal bars, the app reads the battery level and charging state, the Wi-Fi signal strength and the mobile signal strength from Android. This information is used only on your device to draw the icons and the Battery Info screen. It is never stored or sent to us.

Your choices

Your styles, colors, pets, notch settings and the list of apps where overlays are hidden are saved only on your device (and in your Android device backup, if you have it turned on).

Advertising

The HUAWEI AppGallery version of Emoji Battery is free and shows ads served by Huawei's Petal Ads (HUAWEI Ads Kit) inside the app only; ads are never shown on your status bar or over other apps, and no ad is shown when the app starts. The app requests non-personalized ads only, so ads are not based on your interests. To deliver and measure ads, the Petal Ads SDK may process data such as the OAID (Open Anonymous Device Identifier), IP address, device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Huawei's privacy statement: consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy. You can reset or disable the OAID in your phone's settings.

Buying Emoji Battery Pro or Remove Ads turns all ads off.

In-app purchases

Two optional one-time purchases are sold through HUAWEI In-App Purchases: Emoji Battery Pro (screen pets, every premium battery style and all pets, no ads) and Remove Ads (no ads). Payment is handled entirely by Huawei with your HUAWEI ID; we never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation of which product you own, and keeps that on your device so it works offline. Use "Restore purchase" in Settings after reinstalling.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising and purchase data handled by Huawei as described above.

Permissions

  • Accessibility Service – to draw the emoji battery, status bar, notch decoration, pets and charging animation over other apps, as described above.
  • Internet and network state – to load ads and to check your purchases with HUAWEI In-App Purchases.
  • Wi-Fi state – to draw the Wi-Fi signal bars on the custom status bar.
  • App list (launcher apps only) – to show the "Hide in these apps" list. The list is read on your device only.

The app does not request access to your photos, contacts, messages, call log, microphone, camera or location.
