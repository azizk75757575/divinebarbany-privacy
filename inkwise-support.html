Frequently asked questions

Why does Inkwise ask to download a handwriting model?
Recognition runs on your tablet. Each language needs a one-time download of about 20 MB (Wi-Fi recommended). After that, converting works offline. You can manage languages in Settings → Handwriting languages.

How do I get headings, lists, bold text and tables?
Write a title larger than the rest, start list lines with a dot, a dash or a number, underline words to make them bold, and draw a grid for a table. Then tap Convert to text.

What is live writing?
Turn on Auto-convert (menu ⋮ in a notebook, or Settings → Writing). When you pause, your handwriting turns into typed text on the lines at the top of the page and you keep writing below. A full page continues on the next one. Tap Undo in the message to bring your handwriting back.

My finger doesn't draw any more.
Once a stylus is used, fingers scroll and zoom so your palm can rest on the screen. Change this in Settings → Finger drawing.

How do I get a Word or PDF file?
Tap the share icon in a notebook, choose Word (.docx), PDF or text, then Share or Save.

Where are my notes stored?
Only on your device. Inkwise has no account and no cloud; uninstalling the app deletes the notes, so export anything you want to keep first.
