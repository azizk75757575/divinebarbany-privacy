Audio Cutter: MP3 Music Editor – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore)

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Audio Cutter lets you cut, trim and edit songs and the sound of videos on your Fire tablet or Fire TV: make ringtones, join and mix tracks, change speed, pitch and volume, reverse audio and convert to MP3, M4A, AAC or WAV. All editing happens on your device.

No ads, no internet, no purchases

The Amazon Appstore version of Audio Cutter shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, has no in-app purchases and does not request internet access. It does not send any data anywhere.

Your files

The app lists the songs (and, for the Video to Audio tool, the videos) on your device so you can choose them with the touch screen or the Fire TV remote. On tablets you can also pick files with the system file picker. The app only reads the files you select, only to play and edit them, and never uploads them. Saved files are stored on your device in the Music/Audio Cutter folder, where you can play, rename, share or delete them. The app does not download music or videos from the internet. Two short sample files (a song and a video) are bundled with the app so every tool can be tried right away.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics and no location tracking. Your settings are stored only on your device.

Permissions

  • Music and audio, videos – to list the songs and videos on your device so you can choose them, also on Fire TV, which has no file picker. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Modify system settings – optional, asked only when you choose "Set as ringtone" on a tablet, so the app can set your ringtone, notification or alarm sound. Not used on Fire TV.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to save edited audio in the Music folder.

Children

Audio Cutter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Open source

MP3 encoding uses the LAME library (lame.sourceforge.io), licensed under the GNU LGPL.
