Grainbox – Retro Film Camera – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Grainbox is a camera app that gives your photos the look of analog film (grain, light leaks, colour grading and a date stamp). It can also apply these film looks to photos you pick from your gallery. All photo processing happens on your device.

Your photos

Photos you take with Grainbox are stored inside the app until their roll is "developed", then copied to your device's gallery in the Pictures/Grainbox folder. With "Develop old photos" you choose pictures yourself with the system photo picker; the app only reads the pictures you select, only to apply the film look, and saves the result on your device. Photos are never uploaded, and we never see them. Uninstalling the app deletes the photos still inside it; copies in your gallery stay until you delete them.

Camera

The camera is used only while the app is open on the camera screen, to show the live viewfinder and take the pictures you shoot. Nothing from the camera is recorded or sent anywhere else.

Advertising

Grainbox is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Premium films and rewarded videos

Grainbox has no in-app purchases. You can unlock the premium film stocks for your next 10 photos by watching a short rewarded video. Your remaining premium photos and your settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Camera – to take photos.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Taking and developing photos works offline.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to save developed photos in the Pictures folder.

Grainbox does not request access to your whole photo library: it only sees the pictures you pick.

Children

Grainbox is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
