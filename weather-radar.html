Weather Forecast & Rain Radar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps · Applies to the app on Google Play and on the Amazon Appstore (Fire tablets and Fire TV)

Weather Forecast & Rain Radar shows the current weather, an hourly and 14-day forecast, air quality and a live rain radar map for your location or for any city you search for.

Your location

If you choose “Use my location”, the app asks for Android's location permission. Your position is used only to fetch the weather where you are: the app sends the coordinates to the Open-Meteo weather service and requests the radar and map images for that area. Turning the coordinates into a place name is done by your device's own geocoder. The app does not keep a history of your movements; it stores only the last known place on your device so it can show a forecast the next time you open it.

On Fire TV the location option is not offered; you search for a city instead. On Fire tablets the location comes from the device's Wi-Fi location service. You can withdraw the location permission at any time in your device settings and keep using the app with searched cities.

Weather data services

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) – forecasts, air quality and city search. It receives the coordinates of the places you view and the text you type in the city search.
  • RainViewer (rainviewer.com) – radar images.
  • CARTO (carto.com), map data © OpenStreetMap contributors – map images.

Like any website, these services receive your IP address and the area of the map or forecast requested. They do not receive any account, name or advertising identifier from the app.

Saved places, settings and alerts

Your saved cities, units, theme, language and the last downloaded forecasts are stored only on your device. If you turn on rain alerts, the app checks the forecast for your place in the background about once an hour and shows a notification shortly before rain is expected. You can turn rain alerts off in Settings.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads on phones and tablets. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, with Google AdMob and Unity Ads as ad networks. To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, these SDKs may collect and process the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, information about installed apps, interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by their own policies:

  • Unity / ironSource: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The ad SDKs do not receive your saved places or the cities you search for. On Fire TV the app shows no ads at all. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of interest-based ads, in your device settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is described above.

Permissions

  • Location (optional) – to show the weather where you are.
  • Notifications (optional) – for rain alerts.
  • Internet and network state – to download the forecast, radar and map, and to load ads.
  • Advertising ID – used by the ad networks on phones and tablets.

The app does not request access to your contacts, messages, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Children

Weather Forecast & Rain Radar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
