Anti Theft Alarm & Find Phone – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Anti Theft Alarm sounds a loud alarm when your phone is moved, taken out of a pocket, unplugged from its charger, disconnected from headphones or Wi-Fi, and lets you find your phone by clapping or whistling. Everything works on your device.

Sensors and microphone

Only while a protection mode you switched on is running, the app reads the motion sensor (Don't Touch My Phone), the proximity sensor (Pocket Mode), the charging, headphone and Wi-Fi state, and – for Clap to Find and Whistle to Find only – the microphone. Sound from the microphone is analysed in memory, a few milliseconds at a time, to recognise a clap or a whistle. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. None of this data leaves your phone.

PIN

The optional 4-digit PIN that stops the alarm is stored on your device only, as a one-way hash. You can also stop the alarm with your phone's own screen lock; the app never sees your screen lock, fingerprint or face data – Android checks them and only tells the app whether the check succeeded.

Advertising

Anti Theft Alarm is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

Anti Theft Alarm has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or unlock extra alarm sounds for the current session, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Microphone – only for Clap to Find and Whistle to Find, analysed on the device, never recorded.
  • Notifications and foreground service – to keep the protection you switched on running with the screen off and to show that it is active.
  • Vibration, wake lock, flashlight – to vibrate, turn the screen on and blink the flashlight when the alarm rings.
  • Display over other apps – optional, only if you allow it, so the stop screen opens by itself when the alarm rings.
  • Internet – only to load ads and the consent message. The alarms work offline.

The app does not access your photos, contacts, messages, call log or location.

Children

Anti Theft Alarm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
