DozeGuard: Video Sleep Timer – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore)

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps · Package: com.divinebarbany.autosleep · Fire tablets and Fire TV

DozeGuard stops the video and locks or turns off the screen when you fall asleep while watching. On Fire tablets it notices that you stopped touching the device and that it lies still while a video app you chose is open. On Fire TV it asks "Still watching?" after the continuous playback time you choose, and if nobody presses a button on the remote, it stops the video. Everything runs only on your device.

No ads, no tracking

The Amazon Appstore version of DozeGuard contains no advertising and no analytics, and it does not use any advertising identifier. We (the developer) do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no account system.

Usage access (which app is on screen)

With your permission ("Usage access"), DozeGuard reads, on your device and in real time, which app is in the foreground and when it was last used, only to know whether one of the video apps you selected is open. This information never leaves your device. DozeGuard keeps only a small log on the device of when it locked the screen and in which app ("Sleep history"); you can clear it at any time, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app. On Fire TV, DozeGuard only checks whether media is playing.

Motion sensor and touch detection (Fire tablets)

While a selected video app is on screen, DozeGuard reads the accelerometer to tell whether the device is held or lying still; readings are processed instantly in memory and never stored. If you allow "Display over other apps", an invisible 1-pixel window is told only that the screen was touched; DozeGuard never records where you touched or what you typed. The same permission shows the "Still awake?" / "Still watching?" card over the video.

Screen lock (Device administrator)

DozeGuard uses the Device administrator permission for a single policy: locking the screen ("force-lock"). It cannot read your data, change your password or erase anything. You can remove it at any time in DozeGuard > Settings; it must be removed before uninstalling the app. On Fire TV this permission is optional.

Purchases

DozeGuard Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Amazon In-App Purchasing. We never see your payment details; Amazon only tells the app whether Pro was bought, and the purchase is governed by Amazon's privacy notice.

Permissions

  • Usage access – which app is on screen and when it was last used, only on the device.
  • Device administrator (lock screen only) – to lock the screen when you have fallen asleep.
  • Display over other apps (optional) – touch detection and the warning card.
  • Foreground service and notifications – a silent notification while the sleep guard is on; no work is done while the screen is off.
  • Run at startup – to turn the guard back on after a restart, if you had it on.
  • Vibration – a soft vibration with the warning on tablets.

Children

DozeGuard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
