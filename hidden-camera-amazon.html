Hidden Camera Detector – Privacy Policy

Amazon Appstore version for Fire tablets and Fire TV · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Hidden Camera Detector helps you check a room for hidden cameras and item trackers with your device's Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, camera and magnetic sensor. All detection happens on your device. This version contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, sends nothing to us or to any third party, and does not sell or share any data.

Camera images

The Lens Finder and IR Detector (tablets) show the live camera image and analyse it on the device to highlight small bright points. No photo or video is recorded, saved or uploaded.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scans

The Wi-Fi scanner contacts the devices on the local network your device is connected to (network ports, ONVIF and UPnP discovery, device names) to recognise cameras. On tablets, the Bluetooth scanner reads the signals nearby devices broadcast. The device lists stay in memory on your device while the app runs and are never transmitted. On Fire OS versions that require it, the location permission is used only to allow the Bluetooth scan; your location is not read or stored.

Stored on your device

Your room checklist ticks and your sound and vibration settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Camera – Lens Finder and IR Detector on tablets, asked when you open them.
  • Nearby devices or Location (older Fire OS) – Bluetooth scan on tablets only, asked when you start it.
  • Wi-Fi state and multicast – to find the local network and receive camera discovery replies.
  • Internet – required by Android to talk to devices on your local network. The app does not contact any server.
  • Vibration – optional alerts while detecting.

Children

Hidden Camera Detector is not directed at children under 13.
