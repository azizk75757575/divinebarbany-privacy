Home Workout & Fitness Plan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Home Workout & Fitness Plan offers a 28-day home workout plan, quick workouts with animated exercises and a voice coach, Body Check (a workout adapted to your energy, aches and noise level), progress tracking and a daily reminder.

Your workout and body data stays on your device

Your training level, workout history, plan progress, weight and height (used only to estimate calories and show your BMI), Body Check answers and settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app has no account system. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Body Check answers (energy level, which areas hurt, quiet mode) are used only on your device, for the current workout, to swap exercises. They are not saved and not shared.

Voice coach

The voice coach uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine to read exercise names aloud. The app does not use the microphone and records nothing.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your workout and health data is never shared with the ads SDK.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. Body Check unlocks for one workout after you choose to watch a short rewarded video, and you can turn ads off for 24 hours the same way. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no analytics of its own. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Workouts work offline.
  • Notifications – optional, asked only when you turn on the daily reminder.
  • Run at startup – so the daily reminder is scheduled again after your phone restarts.

Health notice

The app gives general fitness guidance and is not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise programme, especially if you have an injury or a medical condition.

Children

Home Workout & Fitness Plan is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
