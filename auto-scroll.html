Auto Scroll – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Auto Scroll scrolls the screen for you: it swipes to the next video, scrolls text smoothly or turns pages while you watch or read in another app.

Accessibility service

Auto Scroll uses the Android Accessibility API (AccessibilityService) for two things only:

  • to perform scroll and swipe gestures on your screen while you have started auto scroll, and
  • to know which app is in front (its package name), so it can pause on the home screen, in other apps and during calls, and stop when the screen locks ("Safe Stop").

The service is configured so that it cannot read screen content (no text, images or input fields). It does not collect, store, log or share any data through the Accessibility API, and nothing it sees leaves your device. You turn the service on yourself after an in-app explanation, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings or from the app's Settings screen.

Wave Control (proximity sensor)

Wave Control reads the phone's proximity sensor (near / far) while a scroll session runs, to recognise a hand wave or a covered sensor. It does not use the camera or the microphone, needs no permission, and the sensor values are processed instantly on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising

Auto Scroll is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

Auto Scroll has no in-app purchases. You can unlock Wave Control for 60 minutes, or turn ads off for 24 hours, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. Your settings (mode, speed, panel size, timers) are stored only on your device. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service – to perform the scroll gestures and pause in the right places, as described above.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Auto scrolling itself works offline.
  • Vibration – a short haptic tick when a wave is recognised.

Children

Auto Scroll is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
