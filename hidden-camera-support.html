Frequently asked questions

Why does the Wi-Fi scan find only my router?
The scan lists devices on the network your device is connected to. Some hotel and guest networks isolate devices from each other, so cameras on them cannot be seen by any app. Combine the scan with the room checklist and, on a tablet, the lens and IR tools.

Why is a device flagged as a possible camera?
Open it in the list: the app shows every reason, for example an open RTSP video streaming port or a camera brand name. It is a hint where to look, not proof.

Why is there no lens finder or Bluetooth scan on Fire TV?
A TV has no camera or magnetic sensor, and the Bluetooth scan is offered on tablets only. On Fire TV the app scans the Wi-Fi network and offers the room checklist; the other tools are on Fire tablets.

Why does the magnetic detector say my device has no sensor?
Some Fire tablets have no magnetometer. The other tools work normally.

Can the app find every hidden camera?
No app can. A camera that records to a memory card sends no signal. Use the tools together with a careful look around.
