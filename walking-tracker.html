Walking Tracker - Step Counter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Walking Tracker is a step counter and pedometer. It counts your daily steps with your phone's step sensor and records the walks you start, including Turn-Back Walk, which tells you when to turn around and guides you back to your start point.

Your steps, walks and routes stay on your phone

Your daily steps, your profile (step goal, height, weight, units) and your walks (time, distance, steps, calories, brisk minutes and GPS route) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We have no server and no account system.

You can save all of this data to a backup file with Settings > Back up to a file, and restore it with Settings > Restore from a file. The backup file is written where you choose, and the app does not send it anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by the app.

Location

Precise location (GPS) is used only while a walk with "GPS route and arrow back to start" turned on is running. It measures the distance you walk, draws your route and points the arrow back to your start. A notification is shown for as long as the walk runs, and location stops as soon as you finish the walk. The app does not use location in the background at any other time, and your locations are never shared or uploaded.

Physical activity and sensors

The "Physical activity" permission lets the app read the phone's step counter sensor. The step count, the compass (to point the arrow back to your start) and the accelerometer (on phones without a step sensor) are processed on the device only.

Advertising

Walking Tracker is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. No ads are shown while a walk is running. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your steps, walks, routes and GPS locations are never given to the ads SDK.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

Walking Tracker has no in-app purchases and no subscription. Turn-Back Walk unlocks for one walk after you watch a short rewarded video, and you can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching one rewarded video. The ad-free period is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and the app has no analytics of its own. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Physical activity – to count your steps with the step sensor.
  • Location (precise and approximate) – only during walks with GPS turned on, for the distance, the route and the arrow back to your start.
  • Notifications – to show the walk in progress (with Pause and Finish) and the optional daily reminder.
  • Foreground service (health, location) – keeps a walk you started running with the screen off.
  • Run at startup – to keep counting steps correctly after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration – to alert you when it is time to turn back.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Step counting and walks work offline.

Health information

Walking Tracker is a fitness app, not a medical device. Distance and calories are estimates based on your height and weight. Do not use it to diagnose or treat any health condition.

Children

Walking Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
