Voice Keyboard – Voice Typing – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Voice Keyboard is an Android keyboard (input method) that lets you type with your voice or with keys in more than 60 languages, and includes a Voice Notepad for longer texts.

What you type

Like every keyboard, Android shows a standard warning that a keyboard "may be able to collect all the text you type". Voice Keyboard does not store, log, upload or share anything you type or dictate in other apps. Text goes only into the field you are typing in. The keyboard is not used in password fields for voice input, and it has no clipboard history, no cloud sync and no account.

Voice typing and the microphone

The microphone is used only while voice typing is active (after you tap the mic, or while Non-stop Dictation is on). Voice Keyboard does not record or save audio. Speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service installed on your phone (usually the Google app / Speech Services by Google), which may process your voice on the device or on its own servers according to its provider's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can download offline speech languages in your phone's settings so recognition stays on the device.

Voice Notepad

Notes you save in the Voice Notepad are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them. You can share a note yourself with the Share button.

Advertising

Voice Keyboard is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob inside the app's own screens (never inside the keyboard while you type). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

Voice Keyboard has no in-app purchases. You can unlock Non-stop Dictation for 30 minutes by watching a short rewarded video. The unlock time is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above and the speech processed by your phone's speech service while you dictate.

Permissions

  • Microphone – for voice typing, used only while you dictate.
  • Internet and network state – to load ads and the consent message, and so your phone's speech service can work online. Typing with keys works offline.

Children

Voice Keyboard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
