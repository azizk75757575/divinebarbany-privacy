Volume Booster: Sound & Bass – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Volume Booster makes the sound of your phone louder. It adds a volume boost, a 5-band equalizer, a bass booster, 3D surround and a volume mixer for the media, ringtone, notification, alarm, call and system volumes.

Audio

Volume Booster uses Android's own audio effects (loudness enhancer, equalizer, bass boost and virtualizer) to change how sound plays on your phone. The app does not record, listen to, store or send any audio. It does not use the microphone and does not read which songs, videos or apps you play.

Background service and notification

While the booster is on, the app runs a foreground service with a visible notification, so that your boost and equalizer keep working in other apps. You start and stop it yourself in the app, in the notification or with the Quick Settings tile. The notification is shown on your phone only.

Settings stored on your phone

Your boost level, equalizer settings and volume choices are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising

Volume Booster is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Super Boost and rewarded videos

Volume Booster has no in-app purchases. Super Boost (boost above 50%) and Bass & 3D open for 30 minutes after you watch a short rewarded video. The unlock time is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Modify audio settings – to apply the boost and equalizer and to change the volumes in the volume mixer.
  • Foreground service and notifications – to keep the boost working while you use other apps and to show the control notification.
  • Internet and network state – for ads only.

The app does not request access to your microphone, location, photos, contacts, messages, call log or camera.

Hearing safety

Listening at a high volume for a long time can damage your hearing and small speakers. The app shows a warning the first time you go above 60%.

Children

Volume Booster is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
