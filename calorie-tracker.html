AI Calorie Tracker: Food Scan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

AI Calorie Tracker helps you log what you eat. You can photograph a meal and let AI estimate its calories and macros, log only what you really ate with Real Bite (a photo before and after eating), split a shared dish with Shared Plate, scan a barcode, search foods, describe a meal in words, or add calories by hand. It also tracks water, weight and BMI. There is no account and no sign-in.

Your diary stays on your phone

Your profile (sex, age, height, weight, target weight, activity level and goal), your daily calorie and water goals, your meals, meal photos, water and weight entries are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We (the developer) do not have a server that receives them, and we never see, sell or share them. You can delete any meal in the app, and you can delete all of your data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

AI analysis (Google Gemini via Firebase)

When you choose to analyze a meal photo (AI Food Scan, Real Bite or Shared Plate) or a meal description, the photo(s) or the text you typed, together with the app's language and the number of people for a shared dish, are sent over an encrypted connection to Google's Gemini model through Google Firebase AI Logic, only to return the food names, weights, calories and macros. Your profile, weight and diary are never sent. We do not store these photos or texts on any server. Google processes them as our service provider under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Do not photograph people, documents or anything other than food.

To protect this service from abuse, the app uses Firebase App Check (with Google Play Integrity), which checks that requests come from the genuine app on a genuine device, and Firebase Remote Config, which tells the app which AI model to use. These Firebase services use a Firebase installation identifier and basic device information. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Barcode scan and food search (Open Food Facts)

The barcode scanner is provided by Google Play services and reads the code on your device; the camera image is not sent to us. To find the product, the barcode number, or the words you type in food search, are sent to the free public database Open Food Facts, which returns the nutrition values. No other information about you is sent. Open Food Facts data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. Meal photos, your diary and your profile are never shared with advertisers. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

The app has no subscription and no in-app purchases. Each AI photo analysis unlocks by watching one short rewarded video, and you can turn banner and pop-up ads off for 24 hours by watching a rewarded video in Settings. These unlocks are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and the app has no analytics of its own. The only data that leaves your device is described above: photos and meal descriptions you choose to analyze (Google Gemini, processed only to give you the result), barcodes and search words (Open Food Facts), the App Check and Remote Config requests (Firebase), and the advertising data processed by Google AdMob. All of it is sent encrypted.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – for AI analysis, food search, barcode lookup, ads and the consent message. Your diary works offline.
  • Camera – the app does not request the camera permission. Meal photos are taken with your phone's camera app, and barcodes are read by the Google Play services scanner.
  • Photos – the app does not request access to your photo library. When you choose "Gallery", Android's photo picker shows only the photo you pick to the app.

Health information

Calorie and nutrient values are AI and database estimates for general wellness only. They are not medical advice, and the app is not a medical device. Ask a doctor or dietitian before major diet changes, if you are pregnant, or if you have a medical condition.

Children

AI Calorie Tracker is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from children.
