Privacy Policy - Cozy Corner Store: Home & Shop

Effective date: 24 November 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

Cozy Corner Store is a free shop simulation game. This page explains what information the game handles and why.

What we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location. Your progress (level, coins, stock, decorations and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and receive no personal data from the game.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC), including optional rewarded videos (for example to call the police during a robbery) and full-screen ads between game days. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information, app interaction and crash/diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before ads load, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options inside the game. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Inside the game: Settings > Privacy options (shown where consent is required by law).
  • Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
