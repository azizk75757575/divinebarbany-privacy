AI Note Taker: Voice Summary – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore)

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps · This policy covers the version of the app distributed through the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets and Fire TV.

AI Note Taker ("AI Notes" on your device) records what you say, and uses artificial intelligence to turn it into text, summaries, organized notes, flashcards, quizzes and answers to your questions. You can also type notes or import a file.

Your notes

Your notes (transcripts, typed text, text read from imported files, and everything the AI makes from them) are stored only on your device. We never receive them, and there is no account or cloud sync. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Microphone and recordings

The microphone is used only after you tap Record. The recording is saved in the app's private storage on your device while you record. While recording, a notification shows that the microphone is in use, so a recording can continue when the screen is off; you can pause or stop it from the notification.

When you stop and ask the app to transcribe the recording, the audio file is sent to Google's Gemini API to turn it into text. The app deletes the uploaded file from Google's service right after the text is received, and deletes the recording from your device once its text is saved in your note. If you discard a recording, it is deleted without being sent.

AI features (Google Gemini)

Transcription, AI notes, flashcards, quizzes, Ask AI and file imports use Google's Gemini models through the Gemini API. Only when you use one of these features, the recording, the text of that note (and your question, for Ask AI), or the audio file, PDF or photo you choose to import, is sent to Google to create the result. The app shows this to you and asks for your agreement before the first AI request. We do not store this content on any server of ours. Google processes it under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

AI results can be wrong. You can report an AI result from inside the app with the flag button. A report sends the reported text, the reason you chose, the AI model name and the app version to our Google Firebase project, without any account or identifier, so we can review it. Do not include personal information you do not want to send.

No ads, no purchases

This version of the app shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK. It has no in-app purchases. You get free AI credits every day; the credit count is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics and no location tracking. Data processed by third parties is limited to what is described above: Google Gemini when you use an AI feature, and our Firebase project when you choose to send a report.

Permissions

  • Microphone – to record, only after you tap Record.
  • Foreground service (microphone) and notifications – to keep a recording going with the screen off and show its notification with Pause and Stop.
  • Internet – for the AI features.

Files and photos are only read when you pick them (or share them to the app). The app does not request access to your contacts, messages, camera or location.

Children

AI Note Taker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
