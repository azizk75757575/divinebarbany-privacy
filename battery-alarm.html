Full Battery Alarm 100% Charge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Full Battery Alarm 100% Charge ("Battery Alarm") rings an alarm when your battery is fully charged, low, unplugged or overheating, and tests how fast your chargers and cables charge your phone. All battery readings are made and kept on your device.

Battery data

The app reads the battery level, charging state, charging current, voltage, temperature and health that Android provides. It uses them only to show them to you, to ring your alarms and to run Charger Check. The results of Charger Check and the names you give your chargers are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Advertising

Battery Alarm is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. Charger Check tests are unlocked by watching a short rewarded video. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

The app has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a rewarded video; this setting is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Alarms work offline.
  • Notifications – to show the battery status and the alarm with its Stop button.
  • Foreground service – Android only tells running apps about battery changes, so a small permanent notification keeps the alarm working while the app is closed.
  • Run at startup – to restart the alarms after the phone reboots.
  • Vibration and keep awake – to vibrate and to keep the alarm sound playing with the screen off.

The flashlight blink uses the flashlight only; the app never uses the camera, your location, contacts, photos or files.

Children

Battery Alarm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
