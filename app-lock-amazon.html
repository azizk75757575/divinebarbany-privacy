App Lock (Amazon Appstore) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

This policy covers the Amazon Appstore version of App Lock for Fire tablets. App Lock protects the apps you choose with a PIN, a pattern or your fingerprint (on devices that have one), hides photos and videos in a private vault, and can take an "intruder selfie" when someone types a wrong code. Everything happens on your device.

Your code

Your PIN or pattern, and the answer to your optional security question, are stored on your device only as salted hashes (never in readable form). Fingerprint unlock uses Android's biometric system: the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, it only receives "success" or "failure" from Android.

Which app is open

To know when to show the lock screen, App Lock reads which app is in front through Android's Usage access permission. This is checked on the device, in real time, only to compare it with your list of locked apps. The app does not record, store or send your app usage history. The list of apps you locked stays on your device.

Photo vault

You choose the photos and videos to hide with the system file picker. They are copied into App Lock's private storage on your device and removed from your gallery (Android may ask you to confirm the removal). They are never uploaded. When you restore a file it goes back to the Pictures/App Lock or Movies/App Lock folder; when you delete it, it is erased. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes the vault, so restore your files first. Hidden files are not included in Android backups.

Intruder selfie

If you turn this option on, the front camera takes one photo after the number of wrong attempts you set. The photo is saved only in App Lock's private storage on your device, shown to you in "Intruder photos", and never uploaded. You can delete the photos at any time. The camera is used for nothing else.

No ads, no internet

The Amazon Appstore version of App Lock shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, has no in-app purchases and does not request internet access. It works entirely offline.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. Your code, locked apps, vault and intruder photos never leave your device. No data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

  • Usage access – to know which app is open so a locked app can be covered by the lock screen.
  • Display over other apps – to show the lock screen on top of a locked app.
  • Foreground service – keeps the protection running; Android shows a notification while it is on.
  • Run at startup – restarts the protection after the phone restarts.
  • Notifications – optional: the protection notification and offers to lock newly installed apps.
  • Biometrics – optional fingerprint unlock through Android's biometric prompt.
  • Camera – optional, only for the intruder selfie, asked when you turn it on.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to restore hidden photos to the Pictures folder.

App Lock does not use an accessibility service and does not request access to all your photos: it only handles the files you pick.

Children

App Lock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
