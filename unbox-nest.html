Privacy Policy - Unbox Nest: Cozy Tidy Room

Effective date: 20 November 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

Unbox Nest: Cozy Tidy Room is a free puzzle game for Android in which you unpack boxes and decorate rooms. This page explains what information the game handles and why.

What we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress (unlocked rooms, stars, hints and sound settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information, crash and diagnostics data, and ad interaction data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads load, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy inside the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Inside the game: Settings > Privacy (shown where consent is required by law).
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
