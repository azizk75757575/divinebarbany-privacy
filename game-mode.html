Game Mode: Game Launcher & HUD – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Game Mode is a game launcher and gaming mode manager. While you play a game started from Game Mode it can silence calls and notifications, set brightness, rotation lock and media volume, show a live HUD and a crosshair over the game, and its Lag Detective feature records your connection and device state to explain what caused lag. Everything is processed on your device.

Game sessions and Lag Detective

During a game session the app measures, once per second: ping (the time to open a connection to a public server such as Google DNS, Cloudflare or an Amazon Web Services regional endpoint – no personal data is sent, only a connection is opened and closed), network type and signal strength, the amount of data the phone sends and receives, battery temperature and level, thermal status, screen refresh rate, battery saver and low-memory state. With usage access it also notes which app was in the foreground (to end the session when you leave the game) and which app used the most data during the session.

These session reports are stored only on your device (up to the last 60 sessions). They are never uploaded, and the app never reads anything inside your games. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

Game Mode is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

Game Mode has no in-app purchases. The detailed Lag Detective analysis of a report opens after a short rewarded video, and you can hide ads for 24 hours by watching one. These choices are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no server of its own and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions (all optional except internet)

  • Internet and network state – to measure ping and to load ads and the consent message.
  • Display over other apps – to show the live HUD and the crosshair on top of your game.
  • Do Not Disturb access – to silence calls and notifications while you play and turn them back on afterwards.
  • Modify system settings – to set brightness and rotation lock for your game profile and restore them afterwards.
  • Usage access – to end the session when you close the game and to name the app that used your data during lag. The list of apps you used never leaves the device.
  • Notifications and foreground service – to keep the game session running while you play, with an "End & see report" control, heat alerts and "report ready" notices.
  • Installed apps (launcher query) – to list the games on your phone so you can add them to the launcher.

Children

Game Mode is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
