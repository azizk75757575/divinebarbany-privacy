Frequently asked questions

How do I stop recording on Fire TV?
Press Home on your remote, open Clipwise again and select "Stop & save". You can also set an automatic stop time in Settings.

Why is a video black or silent?
Streaming apps protect their content with DRM, which blocks every screen recorder. Games, apps and menus record normally.

Why can't I record app sound?
Capturing app sound requires Fire OS 8. Some apps also block audio capture.

How do I move videos to my phone or PC?
Open the Transfer tab, scan the QR code with a device on the same Wi-Fi and tap Download.
