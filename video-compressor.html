Video Compressor Size Reducer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Video Compressor makes videos smaller: it compresses them to a size you choose, resizes them, converts them to MP4 and extracts their sound. All of this happens on your device.

Your videos

You choose the videos with Android's own photo picker or file picker, so the app only sees the videos you select. They are read and compressed on your phone. Your videos are never uploaded, copied to a server or shared with us or anyone else. The compressed copies are saved on your phone in Movies/Video Compressor (sound in Music/Video Compressor). The originals are only deleted if you tap "Delete the originals" and confirm it in Android's own dialog.

Advertising

Video Compressor is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

You can remove ads for 24 hours, or unlock compressing more than 3 videos at once for the current session, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Notifications and foreground service – to keep a compression running when you leave the app or turn off the screen, and to show its progress.
  • Wake lock – so the phone does not fall asleep in the middle of a compression.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save the compressed video in your Movies folder.
  • Internet – only to load ads and the consent message. Compression works offline.

The app does not access your contacts, messages, call log, camera, microphone or location.

Children

Video Compressor is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
