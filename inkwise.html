Inkwise Notes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026 · Developer: Divine Barbany

Inkwise is a handwriting notebook for Amazon Fire tablets that turns handwriting into formatted, editable text. Your notes are created, stored and converted on your device.

Data we collect

None. Inkwise has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Your notes

Notebooks, handwriting and converted text are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them. A file leaves your device only when you export it and choose where it goes yourself (for example "Share" to an email app, or "Save" to a folder).

Handwriting recognition

Handwriting is recognised on the device with Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. The first time you convert handwriting in a language, the app downloads that language's recognition model (about 20 MB) from Google's servers; after that, recognition works offline and your handwriting is never sent to a server. As with any download, Google's servers receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) to deliver the model, and ML Kit may send anonymous diagnostic information about its own operation (no notes, handwriting or text). See ML Kit terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – only to download handwriting language models.

Inkwise does not access your photos, files, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Inkwise does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contact

Questions? Visit our support page or email happyplanetgaming@gmail.com.

Inkwise – سياسة الخصوصية

Inkwise دفتر للكتابة اليدوية على أجهزة Amazon Fire اللوحية، يحوّل خط اليد إلى نص منسّق قابل للتعديل. تُنشأ ملاحظاتك وتُحفظ وتُحوَّل على جهازك.

البيانات التي نجمعها

لا شيء. لا حسابات ولا تحليلات ولا إعلانات ولا تتبّع. لا نجمع أي معلومات شخصية ولا نشاركها ولا نبيعها.

ملاحظاتك

تُحفظ الدفاتر والكتابة اليدوية والنص المحوَّل في مساحة التطبيق الخاصة على جهازك فقط، ولا تُرفع إلينا ولا إلى أي جهة. لا يخرج ملف من جهازك إلا عندما تصدّره بنفسك وتختار وجهته.

التعرّف على الكتابة اليدوية

يتم التعرّف على الجهاز باستخدام Google ML Kit. عند أول تحويل بلغة ما، يُنزَّل نموذج تلك اللغة (نحو 20 ميغابايت) من خوادم Google، وبعدها يعمل التعرّف بدون إنترنت ولا تُرسَل كتابتك إلى أي خادم.
