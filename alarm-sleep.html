Alarm Sleep – Alarm Clock & Sleep Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Alarm Sleep is an alarm clock, sleep tracker and sleep sounds app. Alarms, sleep analysis and sound playback all run on your device.

Microphone and sleep recordings

When you start sleep tracking, the app uses the microphone to measure the loudness of sounds in your bedroom during the night (movement, snoring and sleep talk). This analysis happens only on your device. If the "record sounds" option is on, the app saves short audio clips (up to about 12 seconds each) of snoring and talking so you can listen to them in the morning. Clips are kept for your 14 most recent nights and are then deleted automatically.

Recordings and sleep statistics are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded, shared or sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete a night and its recordings at any time from the sleep report, and all data is removed when you uninstall the app. The microphone is used only while sleep tracking is running, which is always shown by a notification.

Alarms, sleep data and settings

Your alarms, sleep sessions, sleep goal and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no cloud backup of its own.

Advertising

Alarm Sleep is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your sleep data and recordings are never shared with ad networks.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

Alarm Sleep has no in-app purchases. Sleep tracking for one night is unlocked by watching a short rewarded video, and you can turn ads off for 24 hours the same way. These unlocks are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Microphone – only for sleep tracking, as described above. Asked the first time you start tracking.
  • Notifications – to show the alarm, the sunrise light, the sleep tracking and sleep sounds controls, and bedtime reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) and full-screen notifications – so alarms ring at the exact minute and appear over the lock screen.
  • Run at startup – to restore your alarms after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service, wake lock and vibration – to keep ringing, sleep tracking and sleep sounds running while the screen is off, and to vibrate for alarms.
  • Internet – only to load ads and the consent message. Alarms, sleep tracking and sounds work offline.

Not a medical device

Sleep results are estimates based on sound and are intended for personal wellness only. They are not a medical diagnosis.

Children

Alarm Sleep is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
