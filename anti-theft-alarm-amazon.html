Anti Theft Alarm for Fire Tablets – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · Amazon Appstore version · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Anti Theft Alarm sounds a loud alarm when your tablet is moved, unplugged from its charger, disconnected from headphones or Wi-Fi, and lets you find your tablet by clapping or whistling. Everything works on your device, and this version has no internet permission at all.

Sensors and microphone

Only while a protection mode you switched on is running, the app reads the motion sensor (Don't Touch My Phone), the proximity sensor (Pocket Mode), the charging, headphone and Wi-Fi state, and – for Clap to Find and Whistle to Find only – the microphone. Sound from the microphone is analysed in memory, a few milliseconds at a time, to recognise a clap or a whistle. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. None of this data leaves your phone.

PIN

The optional 4-digit PIN that stops the alarm is stored on your device only, as a one-way hash. You can also stop the alarm with your tablet's own screen lock; the app never sees your screen lock, fingerprint or face data – Android checks them and only tells the app whether the check succeeded.

No ads, no tracking

The Amazon Appstore version of Anti Theft Alarm contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not request the internet permission, so no data can leave your tablet through the app.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. No data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

  • Microphone – only for Clap to Find and Whistle to Find, analysed on the device, never recorded.
  • Notifications and foreground service – to keep the protection you switched on running with the screen off and to show that it is active.
  • Vibration, wake lock, flashlight – to vibrate, turn the screen on and blink the flashlight when the alarm rings.
  • Display over other apps – optional, only if you allow it, so the stop screen opens by itself when the alarm rings.

The app does not access your photos, contacts, messages, call log or location.

Children

Anti Theft Alarm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
