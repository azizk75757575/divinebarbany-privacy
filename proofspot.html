Privacy Policy - ProofSpot

Effective date: 15 October 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

ProofSpot is a camera that stamps photos and videos with the time and place and signs them on your iPhone so they can be verified. This page explains what the app handles and why.

Summary

  • Your photos, videos, locations, notes, reports and signing key stay on your iPhone. We have no servers and no user accounts.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data as described below.
  • We never sell your data.

Camera, microphone, photos and motion

The camera and microphone are used only while you take a photo or record a video. Captures are saved in the app and, if you keep the option on, added to your photo library (add-only access: the app cannot read your library). When you verify a photo, the iOS photo picker gives the app only the photo you choose; it is checked on the device and not uploaded. Motion sensors provide the camera tilt (to retake a photo from the same angle), the magnetic field and the air pressure shown on stamps.

Location

Your precise location is used while the app is open to stamp and sign your captures, to show the map on the stamp and to guide you back to a saved spot. It is stored with your captures on your device. To show a street address and the stamp map, the app uses Apple's maps and geocoding services, which receive the coordinates under Apple's privacy policy. If you turn on weather, the position rounded to about 1 km is sent to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) to get the current weather; nothing else is sent.

Signatures and verification

Each capture is signed with a key created in your iPhone's Secure Enclave. The key never leaves the device. The QR code printed on a capture contains the signed time, position, GPS accuracy, compass direction, a fingerprint of the picture and the public part of the key. Anyone who receives the photo can read this information. Scanning the code opens a verification page that checks it inside the browser: the code after the "#" in the link is not sent to any server. Do not share a photo if you do not want its place and time to be known.

Reports and exports

PDF reports, CSV and KML files are created on your device. You choose where they go when you share them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of ProofSpot shows ads provided by Google AdMob, never on the camera screen. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Buying ProofSpot Pro or Remove Ads turns off all ads. Your photos and locations are never used for advertising.

Purchases

ProofSpot Pro and Remove Ads are sold through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
