Privacy Policy - Risebound: Alarm & Wake Check

Effective date: 9 October 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

Risebound is an alarm clock that makes sure you are really awake: you complete a wake-up mission, then a short reaction-time test compares you with your own awake baseline. This page explains what the app handles and why.

Summary

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Everything you create in the app stays on your iPhone. We have no servers.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Information stored on your device

Your alarms and their settings, your wake-up history (times, missions, snoozes, alertness scores and reaction times), your awake baseline and the optional intentions you write after waking up are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded by the app. You can delete alarms in the app, or delete everything by deleting the app.

Alarms

Alarms are scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit on your device so that they can ring like the built-in Clock app. iOS asks for your permission first, and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings.

Camera

The Squat and Barcode missions use the camera. The video is analysed in real time on your iPhone (body-pose detection and barcode recognition by Apple's frameworks) and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. For the Barcode mission the app stores only the code value you registered, on your device.

Motion

The Walk mission counts your steps and the Shake mission reads the motion sensors while the mission is on screen. This data is used only to count progress and is not stored or shared.

Purchases

Risebound Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
