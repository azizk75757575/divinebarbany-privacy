Privacy Policy - Ant Hungry Swarms

Effective date: 25 September 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

Ant Hungry Swarms is a free casual game in which you guide a swarm of ants to eat everything on the island. This page explains what information the game handles and why.

What we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts or location. Your progress (level, coins, settings and the "Remove Ads" purchase status) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information and ad interaction data. On iOS, personalized ads are only used if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before ads load and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy Options inside the game. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. You can restore the purchase on a new device from Settings > Restore Purchases.

Notifications

With your permission the game schedules a local daily reminder on your device. It is created and shown by iOS on the device only; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn it off in iOS Settings > Notifications.

App Store review requests

Occasionally the game asks Apple to show the standard rating prompt. This is handled by iOS; we receive no personal data from it.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to change tracking permission.
  • Inside the game: Settings > Privacy Options (shown where consent is required by law).
  • Deleting the game removes all locally stored progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
