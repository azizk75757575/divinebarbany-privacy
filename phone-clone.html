Phone Clone: Switch & Transfer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Phone Clone moves your photos, videos, music, contacts and files from one phone to another, or between a phone and a web browser (iPhone, iPad, computer), over your own Wi-Fi network or a phone's hotspot.

Your photos, videos, music, contacts and files

When you choose Send, the app reads the photos, videos, music and contacts on your phone (with your permission) so you can choose what to move, and you can add other files with Android's file picker. The files you select travel directly from your phone to your other phone (or to the browser you opened) inside your local network. They are never uploaded to us or to any other server, and we never see them.

Contacts are exported into one vCard (.vcf) file that is sent to your new phone; there you import it with your own Contacts app.

On the receiving phone, files are saved in the gallery, Music and Download folders, like any other file on the phone.

Verification and transfer history

To prove each file arrived intact, both phones compute a SHA-256 fingerprint of every file and compare them. To resume an interrupted transfer and to run Safe Wipe Check, the app keeps a list of identifiers of the files already confirmed (file ID, size and date — not the file contents), a random identifier for the installation, and a short summary of the last transfer. This information is stored only on your devices and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Safe Wipe Check

Safe Wipe Check counts the photos, videos, music and contacts still on your phone and compares them with the list above. It also checks whether a short, fixed list of apps that need their own migration steps is installed (for example WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy) so it can show you those steps. It does not read data inside those apps, and none of this leaves your phone. The boxes you tick are saved on your phone only.

Finding the other phone and browser sharing

The receiving phone shows a QR code containing its local network address and a one-time code, and announces itself on your Wi-Fi (mDNS / DNS-SD) with its device name so the sending phone can find it. A phone that did not scan the QR code can only send after you tap Accept. Scanning the QR code uses Google's code scanner provided by Google Play services; the app itself gets no camera access.

Browser sharing runs a small web server on your phone, protected by a random code in the address, only while the sharing screen is open.

Advertising

Phone Clone is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

You can unlock Safe Wipe Check (until you leave its screen) or turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos, music and audio – to list and send the media you choose to move.
  • Contacts – to export your contacts into a vCard file for your new phone.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save received files.
  • Internet, network and Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast – to connect the two phones on your Wi-Fi, find the other phone, serve the browser page, and load ads.
  • Foreground service (data sync), notifications and wake lock – to keep a long transfer running with the screen off, showing its progress and a Stop button.

The app does not request access to your messages, call log, microphone, camera or location.

Children

Phone Clone is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
