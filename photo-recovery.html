Photo Recovery: File & Video – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Photo Recovery scans your device for deleted or lost photos, videos, audio and documents (the system recycle bin, thumbnail caches, hidden folders, trash folders and app caches), lets you preview them and restores the ones you choose. All scanning and recovery happens on your device.

Your files

The scan reads file names, sizes, dates and the first bytes of files (to recognise pictures and videos that lost their extension) only to show you what can be recovered. Your photos, videos, audio and documents are never uploaded, copied to a server or shared by the app. Restored items from the recycle bin go back to their original album; other recovered files are saved on your device in the "Photo Recovery" folder of Pictures, Movies, Music or Download, where you can open, share or delete them. The scan results are kept in memory only and are discarded when you close the app.

Advertising

Photo Recovery is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The ads SDK has no access to your photos or files.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

Photo Recovery has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a short rewarded video. This setting is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • All files access (Android 11 and newer) – the core feature: the deep scan has to read hidden folders, trash folders and caches that the gallery does not show, and copy the files you choose back into your gallery. The app only reads files on your device and never sends them anywhere. If you refuse, the app can only check the recycle bin.
  • Storage (Android 10 and older) – the same deep scan and saving of recovered files on older Android versions.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Scanning and recovery work offline.

Restoring items from the system recycle bin and deleting recovered files always ask for your confirmation in a system dialog.

Children

Photo Recovery is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
