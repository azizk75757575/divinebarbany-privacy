Calendar: Planner & To Do List – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

This app is a calendar, shift planner and to-do list. You can add events and tasks with reminders, set up a work shift rotation, show a second calendar (such as Hijri or Chinese lunar) under each date, and see your day in a home screen widget.

Your calendar data stays on your device

Your events, tasks, shift rotation and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded: the app has no account, no cloud sync and no server of its own. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted. Android's own device backup may include it if you have turned backup on in your Android settings.

Phone calendars (optional)

If you turn on "Show phone calendars" in Settings, the app asks for the calendar permission and shows the events from the calendar accounts on your phone next to your own events. They are read only to display them on your screen: the app does not change, copy, store or send them anywhere. You can turn this off at any time, or remove the permission in your Android settings.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders for events, tasks and shifts are scheduled on your device with Android's alarm service and shown as notifications. The app asks for the notification permission and, optionally, the "Alarms & reminders" permission so reminders ring on time. No reminder data leaves the device.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your events, tasks and shifts are never shared with the ad network.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. The Shift Planner opens after you watch a short rewarded video, and you can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching one from Settings. These choices are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to load ads and the consent message. The calendar, tasks and shifts work offline.
  • Notifications – to show your event, task and shift reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (optional) – so reminders ring at the exact time.
  • Run at startup – to restore your reminders after the phone restarts or the time zone changes.
  • Calendar (optional, read only) – only if you turn on "Show phone calendars".

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
