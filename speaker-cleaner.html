Speaker Cleaner: Water Eject – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Speaker Cleaner plays sound frequencies that help push water and dust out of your phone speaker, and offers sound tools (speaker test, mic test, decibel meter, tone generator and volume booster). Everything runs on your device.

Microphone

The microphone is used only by the Mic Test and the Decibel Meter, and only after you grant the permission. Sound levels are calculated on your device in real time. The Mic Test keeps your 5-second recording in memory only so it can be played back to you; it is never saved to storage and never uploaded. Speaker Cleaner does not record audio in the background.

Advertising

Speaker Cleaner is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), Speaker Cleaner asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid so ads can be removed.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. Speaker Cleaner has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising and purchase data described above.

Permissions

  • Microphone – for the Mic Test and the Decibel Meter only.
  • Vibration – optional vibration boost during speaker cleaning.
  • Change audio settings – to raise the volume during cleaning (and restore it afterwards) and to play the earpiece test.
  • Internet – only to load ads, the consent message and in-app purchases.

Children

Speaker Cleaner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
