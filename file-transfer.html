File Transfer – Share & Send – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

File Transfer sends files you choose between your devices: from phone to phone, and, with Any-Device Drop, between your phone and a web browser on an iPhone, iPad, computer or TV. Transfers go directly from device to device over your local Wi-Fi network or over a hotspot created by your phone. Nothing is uploaded to the internet or to our servers.

Your files and text

You choose the files to send yourself, with the system photo picker or file picker; the app only reads the files you select. Files you receive are saved on your device in the Download/File Transfer folder, where you can open, share or delete them. Text and links sent to your phone from a browser are shown only on the screen and are not stored. While the Receive or Any-Device Drop screen is open, the app runs a small local web server reachable only on your local network and protected by a one-time 6-digit code; it stops when you leave the screen. We never see, collect or store your files.

Advertising

File Transfer is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

File Transfer has no in-app purchases. Any-Device Drop opens for one session after a short rewarded video, and you can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a short rewarded video in Settings. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to transfer files over your local network and to load ads and the consent message.
  • Wi-Fi state and change Wi-Fi / network state – to show your local address, to create a local-only hotspot when there is no Wi-Fi, and to join another phone's hotspot from its QR code.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android 13+) / Location (Android 12 and older) – asked only when you tap "Create hotspot" or join another phone's hotspot, because Android requires it for local hotspots. The app never reads or uses your location.
  • Wake lock – keeps Wi-Fi at full speed while a transfer runs.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to save received files in the Download folder.

The app does not request access to your photo, video or music library: it only sees the files you pick. QR codes are read with Google's code scanner, which does not give the app access to the camera.

Children

File Transfer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
