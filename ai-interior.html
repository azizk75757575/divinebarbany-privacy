AI Interior Design & Remodel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

AI Interior Design & Remodel ("AI Interior" on your phone) creates new design ideas for a room, a house exterior or a garden from a photo you choose, using an artificial intelligence image model.

Your photos and designs

You choose a photo with Android's photo picker, take one with your camera app, or share one to the app. The original photo and every design you create are saved only on your phone, inside the app. There is no account and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app deletes them. A design is copied to your gallery or shared only when you tap Save or Share.

How a design is created

When you start a design, the app sends the photo (resized to at most 1280 pixels), the space type, the style, the color palette and the optional extra details you typed to our AI server. The app shows this to you and asks for your agreement before the first design. The server runs on Modal (modal.com), a cloud computing provider, and uses open-source image models (Stable Diffusion XL with a depth model) to create the design. Nothing is sent to other AI companies.

The photo and the result are used only to create your design. We do not look at them, use them to train models, or share or sell them. They are kept by the cloud provider only as long as needed to deliver the result and are deleted automatically within 7 days.

To enforce a fair daily limit, each request also carries a random install ID created by the app (not your advertising ID or any hardware ID). The server keeps only a daily count per install ID.

Designs are created by AI and can be inaccurate. You can report a design with the Report button. A report sends the job ID, the style and the reason you chose to our server, so we can review the result while it is still available (up to 7 days).

Advertising and rewarded videos

AI Interior is free. Each design is unlocked by watching one short rewarded video, and the app shows other ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. Data processed by third parties is limited to what is described above: our AI server on Modal when you create a design, and Google AdMob.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to create designs and show ads.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save a design to your gallery when you tap Save.

The app does not request access to your camera, photo library, contacts, messages, microphone or location. Photos are taken by your own camera app and picked with the system photo picker.

Children

AI Interior is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
