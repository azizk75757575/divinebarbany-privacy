Hidden Camera Detector – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Hidden Camera Detector helps you check a room for hidden cameras and item trackers with your phone's Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, camera and magnetic sensor. All detection happens on your device.

Camera images

The Lens Finder and IR Detector show the live camera image and analyse it on your device to highlight small bright points. No photo or video is recorded, saved or uploaded.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scans

The Wi-Fi scanner contacts the devices on the local network you are connected to (network ports, ONVIF and UPnP discovery, device names) to recognise cameras. The Bluetooth scanner reads the signals nearby devices broadcast. The resulting device lists are kept in memory on your phone only while the app is running; they are never sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not use your location: on Android 11 and older, Android requires the location permission for any Bluetooth scan, and the app uses it only for that.

Magnetic sensor

Magnetic field readings are shown live and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising

Hidden Camera Detector is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period

Hidden Camera Detector has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a short rewarded video. This setting, your room checklist ticks and your sound and vibration preferences are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Camera – Lens Finder and IR Detector, asked when you open them.
  • Nearby devices (Android 12+) or Location (Android 11 and older) – Bluetooth scan only, asked when you start it.
  • Wi-Fi state and multicast – to find the local network and receive camera discovery replies.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message, and to talk to devices on your local network.
  • Vibration – optional alerts while detecting.

Children

Hidden Camera Detector is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
