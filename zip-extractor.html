Zip Extractor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Zip Extractor opens, extracts and creates archive files (ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR, GZ, ISO and more) and can recover files from damaged archives. Everything happens on your device.

Your files

You choose the archives yourself, with the system file picker or by opening or sharing a file with the app from another app (for example a file manager, WhatsApp, Telegram or your email). The app only reads the files you select, only to open, extract, rescue or compress them, and never uploads them. Extracted files and the ZIP files you create are saved on your device in the Download/Zip Extractor folder, where you can open, share or delete them. Files shown as previews are copied to the app's private cache and deleted automatically.

Passwords (Password Keychain)

If you enable it, passwords that opened an archive, and passwords you add yourself, are saved so the app can try them on the next protected archive. They are encrypted with a key kept in your device's secure Android Keystore, stay on your device, are excluded from cloud backups and are never sent to us or anyone else. You can view or delete them, or turn the feature off, at any time in the app.

Advertising

Zip Extractor is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

Zip Extractor has no in-app purchases. Rescue Extract is unlocked by watching a short rewarded video (one video unlocks one rescue), and you can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Opening, extracting and creating archives works offline.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to save extracted files in the Download folder. On Android 10 and newer the app needs no storage permission.

The app does not request access to all your files, photos or media: it only sees the files you pick or send to it.

Children

Zip Extractor is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Open source

Archive support uses 7-Zip (7-zip.org, GNU LGPL, with RAR decompression under the unRAR license restriction) through 7-Zip-JBinding (GNU LGPL), and zip4j (Apache License 2.0) for creating ZIP files.
