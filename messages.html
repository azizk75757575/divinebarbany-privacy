Messages: SMS Text Organizer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Messages: SMS Text Organizer is a text messaging app for Android. When you set it as your default SMS app, it sends, receives and shows your SMS and MMS messages, and sorts them into Personal, Transactions, Offers and Spam tabs.

Your messages and contacts

Your text and picture messages are stored in Android's own message storage on your phone, as with any SMS app. The app reads them there to show your conversations, and writes the messages you send and receive there. It reads your contacts to show names and photos and to let you pick who to text.

Your messages, phone numbers, contacts and pictures are never uploaded to us or to any other server, are not shared with advertisers and are not used for advertising. Sorting messages into tabs, detecting one-time codes and flagging suspicious texts all happen on your phone, with simple rules that run offline.

Messages and pictures you send go to the recipients through your mobile carrier, like with any SMS/MMS app. Picture messages (MMS) are sent and downloaded by Android's own MMS service through your carrier.

Blocking, scheduled messages and drafts

Blocked numbers are stored in Android's own block list on your device (shared with your phone app). Scheduled messages, drafts, pinned and archived conversations and your theme choices are stored only on your device. Scheduled messages are sent from your phone at the time you chose.

Backup and restore

Backup saves your text messages to a file in the place you choose with Android's file picker (your phone, an SD card or a cloud drive app). The app does not upload the file anywhere itself. Restore reads only the file you pick.

Advertising

Messages is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads never have access to your messages or contacts. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

Messages has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or unlock a premium bubble theme, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Default SMS app: send, receive and read SMS, receive MMS and WAP push – to send your messages, receive new ones, show your conversations and download picture messages.
  • Contacts – to show names and photos in your conversations and to choose recipients.
  • Phone state – to let you choose which SIM card sends a message on dual-SIM phones.
  • Notifications and vibration – to tell you about new messages, with Reply, Mark as read and Copy code buttons.
  • Alarms & reminders, run at startup – to send scheduled messages on time, also after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state – to load ads. Picture messages use your carrier's mobile data through Android's MMS service.

The app does not request access to your call log, microphone, camera or location. Pictures you attach are chosen with Android's photo picker; the app sees only the picture you pick.

Children

Messages is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
