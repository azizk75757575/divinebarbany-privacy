Frequently asked questions

Why can't Clearwise clear other apps' cache by itself?
Android only lets the system clear another app's cache. Clearwise opens each app's settings for you: go to Storage, select "Clear cache", then press Back.

Why does Android ask before deleting photos or videos?
From Android 11, the system confirms every deletion of media files. This protects your photos; select Allow to finish cleaning.

Why are app sizes not shown?
Android needs "Usage access" for that. Open the Apps tab and select Allow.

Will Clearwise delete my photos?
Never without your choice. Large files and duplicates are listed for review and are never selected automatically; for duplicates the original is always kept.

How does the Speaker Cleaner work?
It plays low-frequency tones (165 Hz) and sweeps that push water and dust out of the speaker grille. Place the phone with the speaker facing down and repeat if the sound is still muffled.
