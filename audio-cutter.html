Audio Cutter: MP3 Music Editor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Audio Cutter lets you cut, trim and edit songs and the sound of videos: make ringtones, join and mix tracks, change speed, pitch and volume, reverse audio and convert to MP3, M4A, AAC or WAV. All editing happens on your device.

Your files

You choose the songs yourself, from the app's list of the music on your device or with the system file picker; videos are chosen with the system photo picker. The app only reads the files you select, only to play and edit them, and never uploads them. Saved files are stored on your device in the Music/Audio Cutter folder, where you can play, rename, share or delete them. The app does not download music or videos from the internet.

Advertising

Audio Cutter is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

Audio Cutter has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or unlock 320 kbps quality for the current session, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Music and audio – to list the songs on your device so you can pick one in a tap. The app only reads the songs you open; nothing leaves the device. If you refuse, you can still pick files with the system file picker.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Editing works offline.
  • Modify system settings – optional, asked only when you choose "Set as ringtone", so the app can set your ringtone, notification or alarm sound.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to save edited audio in the Music folder.

The app does not request access to your photos or videos: it only sees the videos you pick.

Children

Audio Cutter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
