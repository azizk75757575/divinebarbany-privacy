Age Calculator: Date of Birth – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Age Calculator: Date of Birth calculates exact ages, birthday countdowns, life milestones, zodiac signs and planet ages from the dates you enter. All calculations happen on your device.

Birthdays you save

You can save your own date of birth and the names and birthdays of family and friends to get reminders. These are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, shared or sent to us. There is no account. Deleting a birthday, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows birthday reminders and milestone alerts. They are created on your device from the birthdays you saved; no server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in your Android settings.

Share cards

When you tap "Share card", the app creates an image on your device and opens the Android share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to share with.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. Some features (Milestone Radar) open after watching a short rewarded video. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The birthdays and names you enter never leave your device. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. All calculations work offline.
  • Notifications – optional, for birthday reminders and milestone alerts.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, location or files.

Children

Age Calculator: Date of Birth is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
