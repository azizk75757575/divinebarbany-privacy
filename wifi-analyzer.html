WiFi Analyzer: Signal & Speed – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

WiFi Analyzer shows your Wi-Fi signal and connection details, tests your internet speed, lists nearby Wi-Fi networks and the devices on your own network, finds the best Wi-Fi channel and lets you share your own Wi-Fi with a QR code.

Location permission

Android shows Wi-Fi network names and nearby networks only to apps that have location permission. WiFi Analyzer asks for it for this reason only, while you use the app. The app does not read your GPS position and never stores or sends your location.

Wi-Fi details, nearby networks and devices

Network names, signal levels, channels, IP addresses and the list of devices found on your network are read on your phone and shown on your phone. The device scan sends standard network messages (ping, TCP connection checks, SSDP/UPnP, NetBIOS and reverse DNS) only inside your local network. None of this information is uploaded to us or to any other server.

Speed test, ping and DNS tools

The speed test downloads and uploads random test data to Cloudflare's public speed test servers (speed.cloudflare.com) to measure your connection; no personal information is included. Ping, traceroute and DNS lookups go only to the address you type. Your speed test history is stored only on your device, and you can clear it in the app.

Share Wi-Fi with a QR code

The Wi-Fi name and password you type are turned into a QR code on your phone. They are not saved by the app and are never sent anywhere. If you choose Share, the QR image goes only to the app you pick. WiFi Analyzer does not show or recover passwords of other networks.

Mobile signal

The Mobile Signal screen reads your phone's signal strength, network type and operator name from Android and shows them on your phone only.

Advertising

WiFi Analyzer is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Pro tools and rewarded videos

WiFi Analyzer has no in-app purchases. Some tools open for 30 minutes after you watch a short rewarded video. The unlock time is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app) and Nearby Wi-Fi devices – required by Android to show Wi-Fi network names and to scan nearby networks.
  • Wi-Fi state, change Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast – to read your connection, start Wi-Fi scans and discover devices on your network.
  • Internet and network state – for the speed test, the network tools and ads.

The app does not request access to your photos, contacts, messages, call log, microphone or camera, and it does not use location in the background.

Children

WiFi Analyzer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
