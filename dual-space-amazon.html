Dual Space for Fire Tablets – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Dual Space lets you run a second copy of apps you already have on your Fire tablet, so you can use two accounts at the same time. The copies run inside Dual Space itself, in a separate space with its own data. Everything happens on your device.

How cloning works

When you clone an app, Dual Space uses the app that is already installed on your tablet and runs a second copy of it inside its own private space. Dual Space never downloads apps and never changes the original app. The copy has its own login, settings and files, kept in Dual Space's private storage on your tablet.

Dual Space never reads what you do in a cloned app: your accounts, messages, photos and files in the copy belong to that app and stay on your device (or go to that app's own servers, under that app's privacy policy). Removing a clone deletes it and its data; "Delete Dual Space" erases all clones and their data. Your original apps and their data are never touched.

While Dual Space is running it shows a small notification, because Fire OS would otherwise close it in the background and your copies would stop.

In-app purchase

Cloning is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase made through the Amazon Appstore. Payment is handled entirely by Amazon; we never see your payment details. Dual Space only receives from Amazon whether this purchase was made (a receipt), so that cloning can be unlocked and restored on your other Fire tablets. Amazon's privacy notice applies to the purchase: amazon.com/privacy.

Advertising and analytics

This version of Dual Space shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting and no servers of its own. The list of your apps, the apps you cloned and everything inside Dual Space never leave your device.

Permissions

A cloned app runs inside Dual Space, so Dual Space declares the permissions that the apps you clone may need (for example camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage). Dual Space itself does not use them: Fire OS asks you before a cloned app uses camera, microphone, contacts or location, exactly as it would for the original app, and you can turn each permission off in Settings > Apps > Dual Space.

  • List of installed apps – to show the apps you can clone. The list is not stored or sent.
  • Internet – used by the apps you clone, and to reach the Amazon Appstore for the purchase. Dual Space sends nothing of its own.
  • Foreground service / notification – keeps Dual Space running so your copies keep working.

Children

Dual Space is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
