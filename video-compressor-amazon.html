Video Compressor (Amazon Appstore) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Video Compressor makes videos smaller: it compresses them to a size you choose, resizes them, converts them to MP4 and extracts their sound. All of this happens on your device.

Your videos

You choose the videos with Android's own photo picker or file picker, so the app only sees the videos you select. They are read and compressed on your device. Your videos are never uploaded, copied to a server or shared with us or anyone else. The compressed copies are saved on your device in Movies/Video Compressor (sound in Music/Video Compressor). The originals are only deleted if you tap "Delete the originals" and confirm it in Android's own dialog.

No ads, no internet

The Amazon Appstore version of Video Compressor contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it does not request the Internet permission: it never connects to the internet.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. No data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

  • Notifications and foreground service – to keep a compression running when you leave the app or turn off the screen, and to show its progress.
  • Wake lock – so the device does not fall asleep in the middle of a compression.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save the compressed video in your Movies folder.
  • Photos and videos (Fire TV) – Fire TV has no system file picker, so the app lists the videos on the device itself to let you choose one. The list is shown only to you and never leaves the device.

The app does not access your contacts, messages, call log, camera, microphone or location.

Children

Video Compressor is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
