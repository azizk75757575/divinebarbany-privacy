AI Note Taker: Voice Summary – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

AI Note Taker ("AI Notes" on your phone) transcribes what you record, and uses artificial intelligence to turn your notes into summaries, organized notes, flashcards, quizzes and answers to your questions.

Your notes

Your notes (transcripts, typed text, text read from imported files, and everything the AI makes from them) are stored only on your phone. We never receive them, and there is no account or cloud sync. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Microphone and transcription

When you tap Record, the app gives the microphone to your phone's speech recognition service (on most phones, Google's speech services) to turn speech into text live. Depending on your phone and language, recognition happens on the phone or through that service's servers, under its provider's privacy policy. AI Note Taker does not save any audio: only the resulting text is kept, on your phone. While recording, a notification shows that the microphone is in use, so a recording can continue when the screen is off or you use other apps; you can pause or stop it from the notification.

AI features (Google Gemini)

AI notes, flashcards, quizzes, Ask AI and file imports use Google's Gemini models through Google Firebase (Firebase AI Logic). Only when you use one of these features, the text of that note (and your question, for Ask AI), or the audio file, PDF or photo you choose to import, is sent to Google to create the result. The app shows this to you and asks for your agreement before the first AI request. We do not store this content on any server of ours. Google processes it under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

To protect the AI service from abuse, the app uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, which checks that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. Firebase may process technical identifiers (such as a Firebase installation ID, the app version and device information) for this purpose and to deliver app configuration (Firebase Remote Config).

AI results can be wrong. You can report an AI result from inside the app with the flag button. A report sends the reported text, the reason you chose, the AI model name and the app version to our Firebase project, without any account or identifier, so we can review it. Do not include personal information you do not want to send.

Advertising

AI Note Taker is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

AI credits and rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. You get free AI credits every day; watching a short rewarded video gives you more credits for the day, or turns ads off for 24 hours. Credit counts and these settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. Data processed by third parties is limited to what is described above: Google's speech recognition, Google Gemini through Firebase when you use an AI feature, Firebase App Check and Remote Config, and Google AdMob.

Permissions

  • Microphone – to transcribe what you record, only after you tap Record.
  • Foreground service (microphone) and notifications – to keep a recording going with the screen off and show its notification with Pause and Stop.
  • Internet and network state – for AI features, speech recognition where it runs online, and ads.

Files and photos are only read when you pick them (or share them to the app). The app does not request access to your contacts, messages, call log, camera or location.

Children

AI Note Taker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
