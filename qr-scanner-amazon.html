QR Code Scanner: Barcode Scan – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore version)

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

QR Code Scanner reads QR codes and barcodes with your camera or from a picture you choose, and lets you create your own codes. On Fire TV it shows the codes you create full screen so people in the room can scan them with their phones.

Camera

The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to read codes. Camera images are analysed on your device by the open-source ZXing library and are never recorded, stored or sent to us or anyone else.

Pictures

To scan a code from a picture, or to add a logo to a QR code you create, you pick the picture with Android's own photo picker; the app only gets the picture you pick and does not keep a copy of it. Codes you save are written to your gallery (Pictures › QR Scanner) only when you tap Save.

Your history

The codes you scan and create are saved in a history file on your device only, so you can find them again. You can delete single codes or the whole history, or turn history off in Settings. We never receive your history. When you export it as CSV or share a code, it goes only to the app you choose.

Link check

Before you open a scanned link, the app checks the address on your device for common warning signs (look-alike letters, fake brand names, short links, unencrypted links and similar). The link is not sent anywhere for this check. A link opens only when you tap Open link, or automatically if you turn on that option in Settings.

Actions on results

When you tap an action (open a link, join a Wi-Fi network, add a contact or calendar event, call, send a message, open a map, search a product or book), the app hands the data to the app or system screen that performs it. Product and book searches open a Google search page in your browser with the scanned number.

No ads, no tracking

This version of the app shows no ads, has no in-app purchases and contains no advertising or analytics SDK. It does not use an advertising ID.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. No data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

  • Camera – to scan codes. Asked the first time you open the scanner; you can still scan pictures without it.
  • Internet and network state – only to open links and searches when you choose to.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save a created code to your gallery when you tap Save.

The app does not request access to your whole photo library, contacts, messages, call log, microphone or location.

Children

QR Code Scanner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
