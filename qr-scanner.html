QR Code Scanner: Barcode Scan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

QR Code Scanner reads QR codes and barcodes with your camera or from a picture you choose, and lets you create your own codes.

Camera

The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to read codes. Camera images are analysed on your phone (by Google ML Kit inside Google Play services, or by the open-source ZXing library) and are never recorded, stored or sent to us or anyone else.

Pictures

To scan a code from a picture, or to add a logo to a QR code you create, you pick the picture with Android's own photo picker; the app only gets the picture you pick and does not keep a copy of it. Codes you save are written to your gallery (Pictures › QR Scanner) only when you tap Save.

Your history

The codes you scan and create are saved in a history file on your phone only, so you can find them again. You can delete single codes or the whole history, or turn history off in Settings. We never receive your history. When you export it as CSV or share a code, it goes only to the app you choose.

Link check

Before you open a scanned link, the app checks the address on your phone for common warning signs (look-alike letters, fake brand names, short links, unencrypted links and similar). The link is not sent anywhere for this check. A link opens only when you tap Open link, or automatically if you turn on that option in Settings.

Actions on results

When you tap an action (open a link, join a Wi-Fi network, add a contact or calendar event, call, send a message, open a map, search a product or book), the app hands the data to the app or system screen that performs it. Product and book searches open a Google search page in your browser with the scanned number.

Advertising

QR Code Scanner is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or add a logo to your QR codes for the current session, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Camera – to scan codes. Asked the first time you open the scanner; you can still scan pictures without it.
  • Internet and network state – to load ads and, when you choose, to open links and searches.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save a created code to your gallery when you tap Save.

The app does not request access to your whole photo library, contacts, messages, call log, microphone or location.

Children

QR Code Scanner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
