Privacy Policy - Pals Dragon Evolution (Amazon Appstore)

Effective date: 31 October 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

Pals Dragon Evolution is a casual game for Amazon Fire tablets and Fire TV in which you launch your dragon from a giant slingshot, glide as far as you can, collect coins and evolve your dragon. This page explains what information the Amazon Appstore version of the game handles.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or any device identifier. Your progress (coins, upgrade levels, dragon stage, best distance and the items you own) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

This version of the game contains no advertising and no advertising or analytics SDKs.

In-app purchases

Optional items (coin packs, the Starter Pack, Golden Wings and Evolve Now) are sold through Amazon In-App Purchasing. Payments are processed entirely by Amazon; we never see or store your payment details. Amazon tells the game which items your account owns so they can be restored on your other devices. Amazon's privacy notice: amazon.com/privacy.

Notifications

The game does not send push notifications.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Deleting the game removes all locally stored progress. Purchases stay on your Amazon account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
