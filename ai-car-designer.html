AI Car Designer: Car Modify AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

AI Car Designer ("AI Car Designer" on your phone) shows how your car could look with new paint, wheels, a body kit, a different stance and other modifications, from a photo you choose, using an artificial intelligence image model. It also shows a Build Sheet with typical prices for the parts.

Your photos and designs

You choose a photo with Android's photo picker, take one with your camera app, or share one to the app. The original photo, every design you create and its Build Sheet are saved only on your phone, inside the app (My Garage). There is no account and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app deletes them. A design is copied to your gallery or shared only when you tap Save or Share.

How a design is created

When you tap "Design with AI", the app sends the photo (resized to at most 1024 pixels) and a text description of the modifications you picked (and any idea you typed) to our AI server over an encrypted connection. The server runs on Cloudflare Workers AI (cloudflare.com) and uses an open image model (FLUX.2 klein by Black Forest Labs) hosted by Cloudflare. Nothing is sent to other AI companies.

The photo and the result are used only to create your design and are returned to your phone. Our server does not store them, we do not look at them, use them to train models, or share or sell them. Cloudflare processes them only to deliver the result and does not use them to train models.

To keep the free service fair, the server limits how many designs can be requested per minute from one network. For this it uses your IP address for a short time only; it is not stored or linked to you.

Designs are created by AI and can be inaccurate. Prices in the Build Sheet are typical estimates, not offers. You can report a design with the flag button on the result screen: the design is hidden, and you can delete it from your phone.

Advertising and rewarded videos

AI Car Designer is free. Each AI design is unlocked by watching one short rewarded video, and the app shows other ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. Data processed by third parties is limited to what is described above: our AI server on Cloudflare when you create a design, and Google AdMob.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to create designs and show ads.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save a design to your gallery when you tap Save.

The app does not request access to your camera, photo library, contacts, messages, microphone or location. Photos are taken by your own camera app and picked with the system photo picker.

Children

AI Car Designer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
