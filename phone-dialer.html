Phone Dialer: Calls & Contacts – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Phone Dialer: Calls & Contacts is a phone app for Android. When you set it as your default phone app, it places and answers your calls, shows the call screen, lists your contacts and call history, and lets you block numbers.

Your contacts, calls and call history

The app reads your contacts to show names, photos and favorites, and to show who is calling. It reads your call log to show your recent and missed calls. All of this is read directly from your phone and displayed on your phone only. Your contacts, phone numbers, call history and call details are never uploaded to us or to any other server, are not shared with advertisers, and are not used for advertising. The app does not look up unknown numbers on the internet.

The app changes your data only when you ask it to: starring a contact as a favorite, deleting calls from the call log, or adding and removing blocked numbers. Creating and editing contacts opens your phone's own contacts editor.

Calls and call blocking

Calls are handled by Android's telephony system; the app shows the call screen and sends your actions (answer, decline, mute, speaker, hold, keypad tones) to Android. The app does not record calls and has no access to call audio. Blocked numbers are stored in Android's own block list on your device. The options "Block hidden numbers" and "Block numbers not in contacts" are checked on your device only.

Quick replies

When you decline a call with a quick reply, Android sends that text message to the caller through your phone's messaging app and carrier. Your quick reply texts are stored on your device only.

Advertising

Phone Dialer is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown on the call screen or during a call. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

Phone Dialer has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or unlock a call screen theme, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Default phone app (call handling), phone, call log – to place and answer calls, show the call screen, choose the SIM, show your call history and block numbers.
  • Contacts (read and write) – to show your contacts and favorites, show who is calling, and save your favorites.
  • Notifications and full-screen notifications – to show incoming calls, also over the lock screen, and the ongoing call.
  • Wake lock, vibration, audio settings – to turn the screen off near your ear during a call, vibrate on keypad taps, and switch the speaker or Bluetooth.
  • Internet and network state – only to load ads.

The app does not request access to your photos, files, messages, microphone recording, camera or location.

Children

Phone Dialer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
