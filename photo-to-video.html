Photo to Video Maker & Music – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Photo to Video lets you make videos from your photos with music, turn a video clip into a Live Photo (Motion Photo) or live wallpaper, and save photos from your videos.

Your photos, videos and music

You choose the photos, videos and music with Android's own photo picker or file picker; the app only gets access to the files you pick. Everything is processed on your phone: videos, Live Photos and frames are made by your phone's own processor and saved to your gallery (Movies / Photo to Video and Pictures / Photo to Video). Your files are never uploaded to us or to any other server. The app keeps a short list of what you made ("My Creations") on your phone, and a copy of each Live Photo clip so it can be used as a live wallpaper. Deleting an item in the app removes it from the list and from your phone.

Live wallpaper

If you choose "Set as live wallpaper", Android shows its own wallpaper preview and you confirm there. The wallpaper plays your clip from the app's storage on your phone, muted; it does not access anything else.

Advertising

Photo to Video is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

Photo to Video has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or unlock Full HD 1080p export for the current session, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to load ads and the consent message. Making videos, Live Photos and frames works offline.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save your videos and photos to the gallery.

The app does not request access to your whole photo library, contacts, messages, call log, microphone, camera or location.

Children

Photo to Video is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
