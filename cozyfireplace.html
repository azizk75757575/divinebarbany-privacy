Cozy Fireplace – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore)

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps · Package: com.divinebarbany.cozyfireplace · Fire TV and Fire tablets

Cozy Fireplace plays a looping fireplace video with crackling sounds on your TV, with a sleep timer and simple remote controls. The video and sound are built into the app and play entirely on your device.

No data collected

Cozy Fireplace does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use any advertising identifier. The app does not send anything over the internet.

Purchases

Cozy Fireplace is free and contains no in-app purchases.

Permissions

  • Network state – requested automatically by the video player library to check the connection type. Cozy Fireplace does not download or upload anything.

The app keeps the screen on only while the fire is playing, so your TV does not switch to its screensaver. Your sleep timer and sound settings exist only while the app is open.

Children

Cozy Fireplace is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
