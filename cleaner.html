Clearwise Cleaner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 · Developer: Divine Barbany

Clearwise frees up storage and memory on Amazon Fire tablets and Fire TV devices. Everything it does happens on your device: the app has no internet permission and cannot send anything anywhere.

Data we collect

None. Clearwise has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Files on your device

To find junk, Clearwise reads the names, sizes and dates of files in your device's shared storage (for example temporary files, thumbnail caches, old app installers, large files and duplicates). Duplicate detection compares file contents on the device. Results are shown only to you and are not saved or uploaded. Files are deleted only after you review the list and choose Clean; large files and duplicates are never selected automatically.

Apps on your device

The App Manager and Memory Boost list the apps installed on your device and, if you allow usage access, their size, cache and when you last used them. This information is read from Fire OS when you open those screens and never leaves your device. Uninstalling an app or clearing its cache always goes through the Fire OS confirmation screens.

Permissions

  • Storage / All files access – to find and remove junk files you select.
  • Usage access (optional) – to show app sizes, cache sizes and last-used dates.
  • Query installed apps – to show the App Manager and detect leftovers of removed apps.
  • Request uninstall – to open the Fire OS uninstall screen when you ask for it.
  • Close background apps – used by Memory Boost; only apps in the background are affected.

Children

Clearwise does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
