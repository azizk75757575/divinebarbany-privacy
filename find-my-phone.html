Find My Phone: Clap & Whistle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Find My Phone helps you find your phone when you cannot see it. Clap twice or whistle and the phone answers with a loud sound, even on silent, with the flashlight and vibration. With Hot & Cold Sonar, the phone answers with short pings that get faster as you get closer.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you have switched listening on. Sound is analysed in memory, a few milliseconds at a time, to recognise two claps or a whistle (and, with Hot & Cold Sonar, how loud each clap is). Sound is never recorded, saved, uploaded or shared, and it is never used to recognise speech or people.

Because the app must keep listening while the phone is locked or the app is closed, Android requires it to run a foreground service of type "microphone". A notification is always shown while it listens, with a "Turn off" button. If you turn on "Pause while I use my phone", the microphone is switched off while you use your phone and switched back on when you lock the screen.

Display over other apps

This permission is optional. It lets the "Here I am!" screen open by itself when your phone is found, even when the phone is locked. It is used for nothing else.

Flashlight, vibration and screen

When your phone is found, the app can blink the camera flash, vibrate and turn the screen on. It only switches the flash light on and off. It never uses the camera to take pictures or video. To stop the sound when you pick up the phone, it reads the motion sensor while the phone is ringing.

Notifications

The app shows a notification while it listens (required by Android) and a notification with an "I found it!" button when your phone is found.

Settings stored on your phone

Your settings (clap or whistle, sensitivity, ringing sound, flash, vibration, ring time), a "phone found" counter and the PRO unlock time are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

Find My Phone is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown on the "Here I am!" screen. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

PRO features and rewarded videos

Find My Phone has no in-app purchases. Hot & Cold Sonar and the PRO sounds open for 24 hours after you watch a short rewarded video, then lock again. The unlock time is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no servers and no analytics of its own. Sound from the microphone never leaves your phone. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Microphone – to hear claps and whistles while listening is on, analysed on the device, never recorded.
  • Foreground service (microphone) – to keep listening while the phone is locked, with a visible notification.
  • Notifications – for the listening notification and the "I found it!" alert.
  • Display over other apps (optional) – to open the "Here I am!" screen over other apps and on the lock screen.
  • Wake lock and vibration – to turn the screen on and vibrate when your phone is found.
  • Internet and network state – for ads only.

The app does not request access to your contacts, call log, messages, location, camera pictures or storage.

Children

Find My Phone is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
