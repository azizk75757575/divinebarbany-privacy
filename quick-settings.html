Quick Settings: Control Center – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Quick Settings: Control Center gives you a Control Center panel, custom Quick Settings tiles for your notification shade and app shortcut tiles. Everything it does happens on your phone.

Settings the app changes

With your permission, the app changes screen brightness, auto-rotate, screen timeout, sound mode and Do Not Disturb on your phone, and turns the flashlight on or off. It reads, only to show them, the on/off state of Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC and location, your battery level and your next alarm time. For Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, airplane mode, NFC, hotspot and location it opens Android's own panel or settings page; it does not change them itself. None of this information leaves your phone.

App shortcut tiles

To let you pick apps for the shortcut tiles, the app lists the apps on your phone that have a launcher icon. The apps you pick are saved on your device only and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Floating Control Center

The floating Control Center draws a small edge button and the settings panel over other apps, using Android's "Display over other apps" permission. It does not read, record or capture what is on your screen or in other apps. It is unlocked for 1 hour by watching a short rewarded video and then turns itself off.

Advertising

Quick Settings: Control Center is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos and ad-free period

The app has no in-app purchases. You can unlock the floating Control Center for 1 hour, or turn the other ads off for 24 hours, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Modify system settings – to change brightness, auto-rotate, screen timeout and Keep Awake.
  • Display over other apps – only for the floating Control Center edge button and panel.
  • Do Not Disturb access – to switch to silent mode and turn Do Not Disturb on or off.
  • Internet and network state – to load ads.
  • Wi-Fi state, Bluetooth – to show whether Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are on.
  • Query installed apps with a launcher icon – to let you pick apps for the shortcut tiles.

The flashlight is used through Android's torch feature; the app does not take pictures or use the camera image. The app does not request access to your photos, files, contacts, messages, call log, microphone or location, and it does not use an accessibility service.

Children

Quick Settings: Control Center is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
