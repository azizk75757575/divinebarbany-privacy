Mazote – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Mazote helps drivers in Morocco find the cheapest diesel and petrol near them, guides them to the station, and lets drivers share the prices they see at the pump. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

Your location

Mazote uses your precise location to show the stations around you, sort them by distance and guide you to the station you pick. While guidance is on, the app keeps using your location in the background through a foreground service with a visible notification, only to update the route and tell you when you reach the station. Guidance stops when you arrive or when you tap Stop.

To find stations and roads, the app sends the area you are looking at (a point and a search radius) to public OpenStreetMap services: Overpass (station list), OSRM (route) and the OpenStreetMap tile servers (map images). These requests contain no identifier of you.

Prices you share

When you send or confirm the prices of a station, the app sends to our server: the station, the prices, your location at that moment with its accuracy, whether a fake-location app is on, and the prices read from a board photo if you took one. The location is used only to check that you are really at the station, which keeps fake prices out. Published prices are shown to all drivers without your identity. Price reports are deleted from our server after 90 days.

Price board photos

If you choose to take a photo of the price board, the prices are read on your device with Google ML Kit text recognition, then a reduced copy of the photo is uploaded with your report as proof and shown to other drivers on that station. Photos are deleted from our server after 14 days. The app uses your camera only when you tap the photo button, through Android's camera app.

Stations you add

If you add a missing station, the app sends its location, brand and optional name. Proposals that no second driver confirms are deleted after 30 days.

Station managers

If you ask for the Official badge as a station manager, you give your full name and phone number. We use them only to call you and verify that you manage the station. They are never shown in the app or shared.

Anonymous identifier

The first time you share a price, our server gives this installation a random token. It is not linked to your name, your phone number, your Google account or your device identifiers, and it is used only to count confirmations, limit abuse and protect the price list. The server also counts registrations per IP address for two days, to stop automated sign-ups. There is no account and no login.

Advertising

Mazote is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob (banners, a native ad in the station list, a rewarded video that unlocks guidance for 24 hours, and an occasional full-screen ad). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the language menu > Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Your guidance unlock time is stored only on your device.

On iPhone (iOS)

The iPhone version works the same way, with these differences. The map and driving routes come from Apple Maps (MapKit): the area shown and the route start and end points are sent to Apple, without any identifier of you. Prices on a board photo are read on your device with Apple's Vision text recognition; the photo is taken with the iPhone camera only when you tap the photo button. During guidance the app keeps using your location in the background (iOS shows the blue location indicator) only to update the route and notice when you stop at the station; it then may send you a notification asking whether the price has changed. Before personalised ads, iOS asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency); if you refuse, ads are still shown but are not personalised with it. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and your ad consent from the language menu > Ad privacy.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not track your location history, and we do not collect contacts, files or any data that is not described above. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our server runs on Cloudflare.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes your token and settings from your phone. Reports and photos are deleted automatically as described above. To ask for earlier deletion of anything you sent, including a manager request, email us with the station name and the approximate date.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate) – to find stations near you, guide you and check that prices are sent from the station.
  • Foreground service (location) – keeps guidance running with a notification while you drive.
  • Notifications – guidance progress and the "you are at the station" message.
  • Internet – stations, map, route, shared prices and ads.

Children

Mazote is for drivers and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from them.
