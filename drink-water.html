Drink Water Reminder & Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Drink Water Reminder & Tracker helps you drink enough water: it calculates a daily water goal, logs the drinks you add, reminds you to drink and shows your history.

Your hydration data stays on your device

Your weight, activity level, climate setting, daily goal, wake-up and bedtime, reminder settings and every drink you log are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and they are not used for advertising. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. If you use Android's device backup, Android may include this data in your own Google backup.

The app is a wellness tool. It does not give medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your hydration data is never shared with the ad SDK.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. You can turn on the Smart Pace feature for 24 hours, or turn ads off for 24 hours, by choosing to watch a short rewarded video. These time windows are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show your drink reminders. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup – to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration – for reminder vibration.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Logging drinks and reminders work offline.

The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health/fitness data from other apps.

Children

Drink Water Reminder & Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
