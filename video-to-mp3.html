Video to MP3 – Audio Converter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Video to MP3 converts videos and audio files you choose into MP3, M4A, AAC or WAV files, and lets you cut, merge and set audio as a ringtone. All conversion happens on your device.

Your files

You choose the videos and audio files yourself with the Android photo picker or file picker. The app only reads the files you select, only to convert them, and never uploads them. Converted files are saved on your device in the Music/Video to MP3 folder, where you can play, rename, share or delete them. The app does not download videos from the internet.

Advertising

Video to MP3 is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ad-free period and rewarded videos

Video to MP3 has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours, or unlock 320 kbps quality for the current session, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet – only to load ads and the consent message. Converting works offline.
  • Modify system settings – optional, asked only when you choose "Set as ringtone", so the app can set your ringtone, notification or alarm sound.
  • Storage – Android 9 and older only, to save converted audio in the Music folder.

The app does not request access to your photos, videos or music library: it only sees the files you pick.

Children

Video to MP3 is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Open source

MP3 encoding uses the LAME library (lame.sourceforge.io), licensed under the GNU LGPL.
