Smart Flashlight: SOS Torch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Smart Flashlight turns your phone's camera LED and screen into a flashlight, with strobe, SOS and Morse code, Shake to Light, and Power Cut Light, which turns the flashlight on by itself when the electricity goes out while the phone is charging.

The flashlight

The app switches the camera LED on and off through Android's torch control. It does not open the camera, take pictures or record video, and it does not request the camera permission.

Shake to Light and Power Cut Light

If you turn on Shake to Light, the app reads the phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to notice when you shake the phone.

If you turn on Power Cut Light, the app watches, while the phone is charging, whether the charger is connected, how bright the room is (ambient light sensor) and whether the phone is moved (accelerometer), so it can tell a power cut from simply unplugging the phone. It also keeps a log of the power cuts it noticed (start time and duration).

These sensor readings are used on your phone at that moment only. They are not stored (except the power cut log), not sent to us and not shared with anyone. The power cut log and your settings stay on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

While one of these two features is on, the app runs a foreground service with a permanent notification so that it keeps working when the app is closed. You can turn both features off at any time in the app.

Advertising

Smart Flashlight is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

You can turn on Power Cut Light for 24 hours, or turn ads off for 24 hours, by watching a short rewarded video. These settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics of its own and no location tracking. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to load ads.
  • Foreground service and notifications – to keep Shake to Light and Power Cut Light running in the background, with a notification that shows they are on, and to tell you when a power cut was detected.
  • Run at startup – to turn Shake to Light and Power Cut Light back on after the phone restarts, if you had them on.
  • Vibration – a short buzz that confirms a shake.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, messages or call log.

Children

Smart Flashlight is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
