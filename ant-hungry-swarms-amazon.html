Privacy Policy - Ant Hungry Swarms (Amazon Appstore)

Effective date: 31 October 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

Ant Hungry Swarms is a casual game for Amazon Fire tablets and Fire TV in which you guide a swarm of ants to eat everything on the island. This page explains what information the Amazon Appstore version of the game handles.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or any device identifier. Your progress (level, coins, settings, items you own and the ant skin you wear) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

This version of the game contains no advertising and no advertising or analytics SDKs.

In-app purchases

Optional items (coin packs, power-ups, ant skins and the VIP Queen Pack) are sold through Amazon In-App Purchasing. Payments are processed entirely by Amazon; we never see or store your payment details. Amazon tells the game which items your account owns so they can be restored on your other devices. Amazon's privacy notice: amazon.com/privacy.

Notifications

The game does not send push notifications.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Deleting the game removes all locally stored progress. Purchases stay on your Amazon account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
