Dual Space: Clone Parallel App – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Dual Space lets you run a second copy of apps you already have, so you can use two accounts on one phone. It creates a "dual space" with Android's own work profile and installs the copies there. Everything happens on your device.

How the dual space works

When you tap "Create Dual Space", Android sets up a work profile on your phone and makes Dual Space its owner. Dual Space sets no restrictions on your phone and does not manage, read or monitor your personal profile. It only creates the space, installs or removes the copies you ask for, and deletes the whole space when you ask it to.

Android shows a notice that data in a work profile is visible to an "IT admin". In Dual Space there is no IT admin and no company: the owner is this app on your phone, and it does not read or send anything from the space.

Cloned apps and their data

To clone an app, Dual Space reads the installation files of the app already installed on your phone and installs the same app inside the dual space; Android asks you to confirm each install. The cloned app then runs on its own, with its own login and data, like any other app. Dual Space never sees what you do in a cloned app: your accounts, messages, photos and files in the copy belong to that app and stay on your device (or go to that app's own servers, under that app's privacy policy).

The list of apps you cloned is stored only on your device. Removing a clone deletes it and its data; deleting the dual space erases all clones and their data. Your original apps and their data are never touched.

Advertising

Dual Space is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob, only inside the Dual Space app itself, never inside the cloned apps. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Dual Space has no in-app purchases. You can turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a short rewarded video; this setting is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The list of your apps, the apps you cloned and everything inside the dual space never leave your device. The only data processed by third parties is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Work profile (device admin of the dual space only) – to create the dual space and own it. No device policies are set on your phone.
  • Install apps – inside the dual space only, to install the copy of an app you chose. Android asks you to confirm each install.
  • Delete apps – inside the dual space only, to remove a copy when you ask.
  • List of apps with an icon – to show the apps you can clone. The list is not stored or sent.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Cloning works offline.

Children

Dual Space is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
