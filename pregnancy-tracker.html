Pregnancy Tracker: Baby Growth – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Pregnancy Tracker: Baby Growth follows your pregnancy week by week from your due date, shows how your baby grows, and gives you a kick counter, a contraction timer, a weight-gain tracker, a blood pressure log, a daily journal, appointments and questions. With Visit Prep it puts what you logged since your last visit into one report that you can share as a PDF.

Your pregnancy data stays on your device

Your due date, baby's name, height and pre-pregnancy weight, weight entries, blood pressure readings, symptoms, moods, notes, kick-count sessions, contractions, appointments, questions and reminder settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, they are never shared with advertisers or the ad SDK, and they are not used for advertising. The app has no account and no cloud sync. You can erase everything at any time with Settings > Delete all data; uninstalling the app or clearing its data also deletes it. If you use Android's device backup, Android may include this data in your own Google backup.

Visit Prep PDF report

The Visit Prep report is created on your phone. The app never sends it anywhere by itself: it is shared only when you tap "Share PDF report" and choose an app or a person in Android's share menu (for example email, a messaging app or a printer). What happens to the file after that is governed by the app you chose.

The app gives general information. It is not a medical device, does not give medical advice or a diagnosis and does not replace your doctor or midwife.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your pregnancy and health data is never shared with the ad SDK.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Rewarded videos

The app has no in-app purchases. You can open the Visit Prep report for 15 minutes, or turn ads off for 24 hours, by choosing to watch a short rewarded video. These time windows are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show the reminders you turn on (new pregnancy week, appointment tomorrow, daily kick count, daily journal).
  • Run at startup – to set your daily reminder again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet – to load ads and the consent message. Tracking and reminders work offline.

The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data from other apps, and it does not use Health Connect.

Children

Pregnancy Tracker: Baby Growth is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
