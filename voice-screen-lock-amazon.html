Voice Screen Lock – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore version)

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps

Voice Screen Lock shows a custom lock screen on your tablet that you unlock by saying your voice password, or with a backup PIN or pattern. It works on top of your tablet's own screen lock and does not replace it.

No data collection

This version of the app has no ads, no analytics, no account and does not request the internet permission. We (the developer) do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while the voice password screen or the lock screen is listening: when you tap the mic, or when the lock screen appears and "Listen automatically" is on. Speech is turned into text by an offline speech recognizer built into the app (Vosk, English). Audio is processed in memory on your tablet and is never recorded to storage or sent anywhere. The app never listens in the background.

Your voice password, PIN and pattern

Your voice phrase, backup PIN and pattern are stored only on your tablet, in the app's private storage. The PIN and pattern are stored as a salted one-way hash. Uninstalling the app deletes them. The app recognizes the words you say, not your voiceprint, and does not create any biometric data.

Wallpaper photo

If you choose your own photo as the lock screen wallpaper, you pick it with the system photo picker. The app keeps a resized copy in its private storage on your tablet. It is never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Microphone – to hear your voice password while the lock screen or setup screen is listening.
  • Display over other apps – so the lock screen can appear when the screen turns on.
  • Foreground service and notifications – while Voice Lock is on, a small notification shows that it is active; the service only waits for the screen to turn off to show the lock screen.
  • Run at startup – to turn Voice Lock back on after a restart, if you had it on.
  • Vibration – optional vibration when you unlock.

Children

Voice Screen Lock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
