Smart Flashlight: Screen Light & SOS – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore)

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps · This policy covers the version of the app distributed on the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets.

Smart Flashlight turns your device into a light: a full-screen white light (or the camera LED on devices that have one), Screen Light in 7 colors, SOS and Morse code, a strobe light, Shake to Light, and Power Cut Light, which lights up by itself when the electricity goes out while the device is charging.

Summary

This version of the app has no ads, no analytics and no account. We (the developer) do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Sensors and charger state

Shake to Light reads the motion sensor (accelerometer) to notice two shakes. Power Cut Light, while the device is charging, reads whether the charger has power, the ambient light sensor and the motion sensor, to tell a power cut from you simply unplugging the device. These readings are used instantly on your device and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Background service and notifications

While Shake to Light or Power Cut Light is on, the app runs a foreground service with a visible notification. You turn both features on and off yourself in the app. When a power cut is detected, a notification tells you and lets you turn the light off. Notifications are shown on your device only.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and the power cut log (the start time and length of each power cut) are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.

In-app purchase

Power Cut Light can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Amazon through the Amazon Appstore; we never see your payment details. The app only receives from Amazon whether the purchase is owned, and keeps that on your device. Amazon's handling of your data is described in the Amazon Privacy Notice.

Permissions

  • Foreground service, notifications and start at boot – to keep Shake to Light and Power Cut Light running in the background and bring them back after a restart.
  • Full-screen notification – to show the screen light when a power cut is detected.
  • Vibrate – a short buzz that confirms a shake.
  • Internet – only used by the Amazon in-app purchasing service. The app itself sends nothing.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts, messages or call log.

Children

Smart Flashlight is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
