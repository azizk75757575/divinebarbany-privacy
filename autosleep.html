DozeGuard: Video Sleep Timer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps · Package: com.divinebarbany.autosleep

DozeGuard notices when you fall asleep while a video app you chose (for example a short-video or streaming app) is open, shows a "Still awake?" warning, and if you do not answer, stops the video and locks the screen. Everything that detects your sleep runs only on your device.

Usage access (which app is on screen)

With your permission ("Usage access"), DozeGuard reads, on your device and in real time, which app is in the foreground and when it was last used. This is used only to know whether one of the video apps you selected is open and whether you are still interacting with it. This information is never sent off the device, shared or sold. DozeGuard keeps only a small log on your phone of when it locked the screen and in which app (shown in "Sleep history"); you can clear it at any time in the app, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Motion sensor and touch detection

While a selected video app is on screen, DozeGuard reads the accelerometer to tell whether the phone is being held or lying still. The readings are processed instantly in memory and are never stored or sent anywhere.

If you allow "Display over other apps", DozeGuard places an invisible 1-pixel window that is told only that the screen was touched; DozeGuard never records where you touched, what you tapped or what you typed, and your touches still go to your app. The same permission is used to show the "Still awake?" card over the video.

Screen lock (Device administrator)

DozeGuard uses the Device administrator permission for a single policy: locking the screen ("force-lock"). It cannot read your data, change your password or erase anything. You can remove this permission at any time in DozeGuard > Settings; it must be removed before uninstalling the app.

Advertising

DozeGuard is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob inside the app's own screens (a banner, an occasional full-screen ad when returning to the home screen, and an optional rewarded video that unlocks Pro features for 7 days). Ads are never shown on the "Still awake?" warning or while your screen is being locked. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no analytics of its own, and your sleep information never leaves your device. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Usage access – to know which app is on screen and when it was last used, only on the device.
  • Device administrator (lock screen only) – to lock the screen when you have fallen asleep.
  • Display over other apps (optional) – touch detection and the "Still awake?" card.
  • Foreground service and notifications – while the sleep guard is on, a silent notification shows that it is active. The service does no work while the screen is off.
  • Run at startup – to turn the guard back on after a restart, if you had it on.
  • Vibration – a soft vibration with the "Still awake?" warning.
  • Internet – only to load ads and the consent message.

Children

DozeGuard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
