AI Resume Builder: CV Maker – Privacy Policy (Amazon Appstore)

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Developer: AlgoTechApps · This policy covers the version of the app distributed through the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets and Fire TV.

AI Resume Builder ("AI Resume" on your device) helps you write resumes and cover letters, check them against job ads and export them as PDF files. It uses artificial intelligence to write and improve text.

Your resumes, cover letters and photo

Everything you enter (personal details such as your name, e-mail address, phone number, city and links, your work experience, education, skills and the rest of your resume, your cover letters and the photo you add) is stored only on your device. We never receive it, and there is no account or cloud sync. PDF files are created on your device and are only saved where you choose (on Fire TV, in the Downloads folder). Uninstalling the app deletes your data.

AI features (Google Gemini)

Writing the summary, writing or improving bullet points, suggesting skills, the ATS check and writing cover letters use Google's Gemini models through the Gemini API. Only when you tap one of these AI buttons, the text of your resume (which includes the personal details you entered), and the company, position and job description you add for the ATS check or a cover letter, is sent to Google to create the result. Your photo is never sent. The app shows this to you and asks for your agreement before the first AI request. We do not store this content on any server of ours. Google processes it under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

AI results can be wrong. You can report an AI result from inside the app with the Report button. A report sends the reported text, the reason you chose, the AI model name and the app version to our Google Firebase project, without any account or identifier, so we can review it. Reported text may contain parts of your resume: do not report text you do not want to send.

No ads. AI Resume Pro

This version of the app shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK. Some features (the AI tools, the ATS check, cover letters and the extra templates and colors) are part of AI Resume Pro, a one-time in-app purchase made through Amazon. Amazon handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only receives from Amazon whether you own Pro, so it can unlock it and restore it on your other devices. Amazon's privacy notice applies to the purchase: amazon.com/privacy.

Pro owners get AI credits every day; the credit count is stored only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics and no location tracking. Data processed by third parties is limited to what is described above: Google Gemini when you use an AI feature, Amazon for the Pro purchase, and our Firebase project when you choose to send a report.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – for the AI features and the Pro purchase.
  • Storage (only on Fire TV with Fire OS 7 / Android 9) – to save the PDF you download in the Downloads folder, because these devices have no "Save as" screen.

Your photo is only read when you pick it with Android's photo picker. The app does not request access to your contacts, messages, camera, microphone, location or your photo library.

Children

AI Resume Builder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
