Privacy Policy - Engine Simulator

Effective date: 23 November 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

Engine Simulator is a free game in which you start, rev, tune and dyno-test virtual car engines. This page explains what information the game handles and why.

What we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. It does not use the microphone: every engine sound is generated on your device. Your progress (money, engines, upgrades, dyno records, settings and the remaining time of an unlocked dyno session) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC), including optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one to unlock the dyno for 10 minutes or to double your offline earnings. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information, crash and performance data and ad interaction data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before ads load and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy Settings inside the game. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Inside the game: Settings > Privacy Settings (shown where consent is required by law).
  • Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
