Privacy Policy - Emoji Battery: Pet & Charging

Effective date: 11 November 2026. Publisher: Divine Barbany.

Emoji Battery: Pet & Charging is an iPhone app with a virtual pet that eats when you charge and grows when you unplug in the healthy battery range, plus emoji battery wallpapers, charging screens, widgets and a Live Activity. This page explains what the app handles and why.

Summary

  • We (the developer) have no servers and no user accounts, and we do not collect or sell personal information.
  • Your pet, its progress and your designs are stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads can be removed with an in-app purchase.

Battery level

The app reads the battery level and charging state from iOS to feed your pet, award experience points and show the charging screen. This information is used only on your device and is never sent to us.

Information stored on your device

Your pet's name, level, experience points, streak, outfits, recent charge sessions, wallpaper designs and settings are saved on your iPhone, in storage shared only with the app's own widgets. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Photos

When you save a wallpaper, the app asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It cannot read or view your existing photos.

Notifications, Live Activities and Shortcuts

Reminders such as "your pet is hungry" or "your pet is full" are local notifications created on your device. The Live Activity and the Dynamic Island show your pet while charging and are updated on your device; no push server is used. If you set up the optional Shortcuts automations, they run the app's own actions on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address (approximate location), device information, interactions with ads and diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app first shows Google's consent message. iOS then asks whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you do not allow it, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your consent at any time with More > Privacy options (where available) and your tracking choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

In-app purchases

Emoji Battery Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove Ads are sold through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit.

Rewarded videos

You can unlock some items by watching an optional rewarded video. What you unlocked is stored only on your device.

App Store review requests

Occasionally the app asks Apple to show the standard rating prompt. This is handled by iOS; we receive no personal data from it.

Credits

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
