Frequently asked questions

Why can't Clearwise clear other apps' cache by itself?
Fire OS only lets the system clear another app's internal cache. Clearwise opens each app's settings page for you: select "Clear cache", then press Back and Clearwise moves to the next app.

Why does the scan only check photos, videos and music?
Some Fire OS 8 devices have no "All files access" screen, so apps can only see media files. On Fire OS 6/7 and on devices with that screen, Clearwise checks temp files, logs and installers too.

Why are app sizes not shown?
Fire OS 7 and later need "Usage access" for that. Open the Apps tab and select Allow. Some Fire TV models don't have this screen; Clearwise then shows install sizes.

Will Clearwise delete my photos or videos?
Never without your choice. Only safe junk is pre-selected; large files and duplicates are listed for review and are never selected automatically. For duplicates the original is always kept.

What does Memory Boost close?
Only apps running in the background. The app you are using, the launcher, the keyboard and apps you switch off in the list are never closed.
